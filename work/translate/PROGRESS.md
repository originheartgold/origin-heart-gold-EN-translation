## Decisions
- Names use mixed case (Bulbasaur, Poké Ball), not Gen 4 all-caps.
- Modern official names from the glossary override US/v3 text.
- **Trainer table (0719) was reorganised by the hack.** The trainer classes in the v4 trdata (a/0/5/5) match the US ones for only 99 of 738 ids, so the US names seeded by position were almost all wrong and have been replaced. Rules used: official English for anime/Origins/Adventures/game characters; English given names in vanilla-localisation style for generic trainers (one English name per Chinese name, so rematches stay consistent); romanised Japanese surnames for Rocket grunts/evil scientists with surname names (Okamoto, Tanaka, …); pinyin for Sprout Tower monks and elders (like vanilla Sage Li/Chow); glossary species names for "Pokémon trainers". Names kept ≤8 chars (≤10 for a few Pokémon/group names; US max is 10).
- **Generic trainer names:** the authority is bank 0719 itself: look up the zh there. Only story-relevant names are listed below.
- Pokédex categories (0803): official English genus for every species (PokéAPI); official US text kept where it exists, including its compressions (FlyScorpion, TinyRaccoon). Limit is 110 px in font 0 (US max). Longer new ones are shortened Gen 4-style (spaces dropped, e.g. GrassSnake Pokémon; or letters dropped, e.g. Gloomdwellr, SecretAgnt, GrassMonky). All 0803 strings carry qa_ignore for `wider_than_zh` (ui-relative check, meaningless here) and for glossary false positives.
- Location banks 0206/0272/0273/0274 now have `"category": "locations"` in the workspace file.
- CJK characters can't appear in English strings (no hanzi in the English font). Credit names and CJK handles must be romanised, e.g. 雁南飞TB → Yannanfei TB. Keep this in mind for any later credits/readme text and for the patch release notes.

- Money: `$` before the amount, as in the US text and as QA expects (e.g. `${VAR:0137:0}`), replacing the zh `…¥` suffix.
- All-caps menu labels keep the Gen 4 US style, including `POKéMON`, `POKéDEX`, `POKéGEAR`, `POKé BALLS`, `POKé CENTER` in caps labels (the US spelling); mixed case everywhere else.
