These Pokémon appear in land encounters on the same **annual dates** each year. Look in the exact location listed below; they do not replace Surf or fishing encounters.

Their chance is normally **1% of land encounters**, not 1% per step. They can also appear as visible Pokémon nearby.

**Leave and re-enter the area after the date changes.** The game reads the date when you enter, so staying there across midnight keeps the old day’s encounters.

**Morning:** 04:00–09:59. **Day:** 10:00–19:59. **Night:** 20:00–03:59. The month and day must still match: July 18’s night window does not extend into July 19.

## Entries that need care

**Diancie:** appears as normal Diancie, not Mega Diancie.

**Volcanion:** cannot be found in this version. Its calendar entry points to Lake of Rage, which has no land encounters.

These entries are separate from the [Wednesday Lake of Rage events](../guide/dungeons-and-common/#lake-of-rage-when-the-calm-day-happens-continues-lake-of-rage-blackthorn-city-and-beyonds-lake-trio-and-calm-day-visitors) and from [visible encounters and shiny retention](../mechanics/#visible-encounters-and-shinies).
