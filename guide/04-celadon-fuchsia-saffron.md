## Celadon Department Store roof: the prize quiz

**Where:** the Celadon Department Store roof. The quiz attendant stands by the top wall, three steps left of the vending machines.

**How it works:** five questions; you only learn at the end whether you got them all.

| # | Question | Answer | Position |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Which move can confuse the target? | **Dynamic Punch** | 4th |
| 2 | Which type is super effective against the most types? | **Rock type** | 2nd |
| 3 | Which Pokémon looks the same whether male or female? | **Pidgeotto** | 3rd |
| 4 | Which move has the highest priority? | **Fake Out** | 1st |
| 5 | What shape is the red marking on Zangoose's chest? | **M shape** | 3rd |

**Reward (choose one, once):** **Doll Set** = 2 Poké Dolls + 2 Fluffy Tails (the Fluffy Tail is what the [Skitty kid](03-vermilion-to-celadon.md#celadon-city-fluffy-tail-for-the-skitty-kid-unlocks-fake-out) wants), or **Poké Ball Set** = 10 Ultra Balls + 10 Great Balls.

**Notes:** a wrong answer costs nothing; try again as often as you like until you win once.

*Source:* script file 792 (script 15, L2295–L3166; flag 1883). Attendant at ≈12,3 on the roof map.

## Celadon Department Store roof: Tony and Mary's grooming (missable)

**Where:** Department Store roof (6F). Two "grooming genius" Breeders, Tony and Mary, work in the left-hand (west) corner of the roof with a queue of customers in front of them. A Kirlia owner standing just above the queue grumbles that Ms. Suzie in the Celadon Condominiums is far better. Suzie is in the Condominiums lobby (1F), in the upper right part of the room.

**Who gets it / when:** from the Route 7 scene with Misty and Erika (which empties Suzie's shop) until you tell Erika that Lawrence is the Collector (the end of the [Gloom search](03-vermilion-to-celadon.md#celadon-city-the-stolen-gloom-story-chain-easy-to-get-stuck)). After that Tony and Mary are gone for good.

**How it works:**
1. Go to Suzie in the Condominiums lobby. She has no customers and offers a **free** massage: say yes and pick any Pokémon. This is the step that arms the roof scene.
2. Go back to the roof and walk up to Tony's queue: step on the spot just right of the Kirlia owner's Kirlia. Tony "paints" a customer's Kakuna, it evolves into a Beedrill and goes berserk.
3. Battle the berserk **Beedrill** (Lv. 20, single battle). You tell the crowd to go to Suzie instead.
4. Mary and Tony reveal themselves as **Jessie and James**. Double battle: Jessie (Arbok 38, Lickilicky 37, Wobbuffet 36, Dustox 35) and James (Weezing 38, Victreebel 37, Gyarados 36, Meowth 20). James lets slip the "Pokémon Enhancer", the drug behind the berserk Pokémon on Route 7.
**Reward:** Suzie's customers come back and from then on her massage is **free, forever**: it fully heals your party and gives the chosen Pokémon +10 friendship (plus Beauty and sheen). Outside the free window (before the Route 7 scene, and after the Gloom search if you skipped the quest) she charges **$2,000** a time.

**Notes:** after the Route 7 scene, stepping in front of Tony does nothing until you've had Suzie's free massage. (Before the Route 7 scene the roof scene is armed from the start, so if you reach the roof that early it plays without the massage, and afterwards Suzie's massage is free too: tested in an emulator. Whether you can reach the roof that early in a normal playthrough hasn't been checked.) Losing to the Beedrill whites you out and the scene replays, so you can retry until the Gloom search ends. Losing to Jessie & James also whites you out, but the quest already counts as done: the scene doesn't replay and Suzie's massage is free from then on.

*Source:* script files 792 (script 21, coord trigger at 4,5 on var 0x40d0 = 0, which is also the var's starting value; Tony/Mary hide flags 1544/1545 start clear; scripts 11, 15; the quest var is set to 2 at @1530, before the Jessie & James battle, and flags 1544/1545 set, 1535 cleared; Beedrill is trainer 462), 794 (script 1, L728/L1387/L758; $2,000 at L788 whenever flag 1535 is clear and 0x40d0 ≠ 2), 186 (Route 7 scene end, @1783/@1819, sets flag 1535 and 0x40d0 = 1, disarming the trigger; 794 @1402 sets it back to 0 after the free massage), 783 L4581–L4612 (closes it). Positions on the roof map: Tony ≈1,8, Mary ≈1,7, Kirlia owner ≈2,5; Suzie ≈10,5 on Condominiums 1F. Emulator (`emu_harness.py guide0107 --case tony`, both ROMs): with 0x40d0 = 0 and 1535 clear Suzie offers the paid massage (494#0); the roof trigger plays the scene, Beedrill 462 and then 460 + 461, and 0x40d0 is 2 (1544/1545 set, 1535 clear) when Jessie & James's battle starts; from that state Suzie offers the free massage (494#36).

## Celadon Condominiums → Silph Co. 5F: Grandma's treats for Shota (Master Ball; missable)

**Where:** an old woman with a Pikachu on **Celadon Condominiums 2F**, and the head of R&D on **Silph Co. 5F** in Saffron. To reach 2F, take the stairs on the right-hand (east) side of the Condominiums lobby; Grandma stands a few steps up from where you arrive, next to a photo album. On Silph Co. 5F, the head of R&D is in the right-hand (east) half of the floor, near the bottom.

**Who gets it / when:** start it any time in Celadon. The head of R&D is only there **after you rescue Prof. Hale in Rock Tunnel** ([Vermilion City to Celadon City](03-vermilion-to-celadon.md)) and **before Sabrina laughs in Pokémon Tower** (the Saffron takeover, [Pokémon Tower: make Sabrina laugh](03-vermilion-to-celadon.md#pokémon-tower-make-sabrina-laugh-the-marsh-badge-trial)). The quest closes for good when Saffron is freed, so **report back to Grandma before Saffron is freed**.

**How it works:**
1. She says her grandson Shota works in R&D at Silph Co. and hasn't come home. Say yes: you get **2 Lava Cookies** and **2 Old Gateaux** to deliver (you keep them).
2. On Silph Co. 5F, ask the **head of R&D** about Shota. Shota worked himself to death at his desk.
3. Go back to Grandma. She asks "Did he like the treats?":

| Answer | Result |
|---|---|
| **"Shota died from overwork"** | she breaks down, then gives you the **Master Ball** Shota secretly made for her |
| "Shota... loved them!" | **2 Rare Candies**; the Master Ball is lost |

**Notes:** one chance: the answer can't be changed, and nothing warns you about the deadline. If you miss the R&D head before the takeover, the building manager (also on 2F, at the left side of the room) later tells you Shota died and Grandma only has a sad line. Freeing Saffron ends the quest whatever you've done, so the Master Ball is gone if you haven't reported back by then. This is the Master Ball that Silph's president calls "the only one in the world".

*Source:* script files 794 (scripts 12, 13, 14; L894, L948, L992, L1108; var 0x40cf; Master Ball only at value 2), 762 (script 7, L886; object 6 hidden by flag 1226), 129 L1141 (Rock Tunnel clears 1226), 17 L12023 (takeover sets 1226), 834 L5994 (liberation sets 0x40cf = 5). Grandma ≈14,37 and manager ≈6,42 are in the 2F part of the Condominiums 1F map (stairs ≈13,10 → ≈13,41; the manager's line 494#23 says "the old lady on the second floor"); head of R&D ≈44,13 on the Silph Co. 5F map.

## Celadon Restaurant: the eating contest (repeatable)

**Where:** the Celadon Restaurant. The receptionist stands at the right-hand side of the room; the five contestants sit at the tables.

**How it works:**
1. Pay the **$2,000** entry fee. You get **10 Sitrus Berries**.
2. Each contestant battles you for Berries (one Pokémon each, singles). You need **at least 5 Sitrus Berries** in your Bag to challenge anyone. Win: they give you 5 Sitrus Berries. Lose: they take 5 (no white-out) and you can challenge them again.

| Contestant | Seat | Pokémon |
|---|---|---|
| Hiker Gunther | bottom row, right | Onix Lv. 37 |
| Magician Shun | bottom row, left | Hypno Lv. 37 |
| Firebreather Wick | far left, middle | Rapidash Lv. 40 |
| Ace Trainer Ingrid | top row, right | Gallade Lv. 37 |
| Ruin Maniac Zed | top row, left | Unown Lv. 40 (Hidden Power) |

3. **The catch:** after beating all five you must have "eaten" your winnings. The receptionist only crowns you if you have **fewer than 10 Sitrus Berries in your Bag**. With 10 or more she just repeats the rules. Eat, toss or give them to your Pokémon to hold until you're down to 9 or fewer, then talk to her.
**Reward:** a Berry Set: one each of Cheri, Chesto, Pecha, Rawst and Aspear, plus **10 Sitrus Berries**. The contest then resets and you can enter again (another $2,000).

**Notes:** if you drop below 5 Sitrus Berries mid-contest, you can't challenge anyone until you get more elsewhere.

*Source:* script file 803 (script 1, L389–L660; scripts 2–6; flags 1527, 1815–1819). Positions on the Restaurant map: receptionist ≈13,7; Gunther ≈10,9, Shun ≈7,9, Wick ≈2,6, Ingrid ≈8,4, Zed ≈5,4.

## Celadon Condominiums roof: the insomniac and the Hypno (after the Rocket arc)

**Where:** a worried man on Department Store **4F** (towards the right of the floor, near the top wall) can't sleep. The rooftop room of the Celadon Condominiums (the old auction house) reopens as a leisure centre once the Celadon Rocket base is cleared.

**How it works:**
1. After the Rocket base, go to the Condominiums roof room. Talk to the man with the Hypno near the top of the room: he offers it as an insomnia cure. From now on the **Hypno heals your party for free** whenever you talk to it.
2. Go to 4F and talk to the insomniac again: he goes to try it.
3. Back in the roof room, he thanks you and gives you **HP Up** (one time).

*Source:* script files 790 (script 7, L412; flags 1536–1538), 798 (scripts 5, 10, 12; flags 1537, 1891; room opens when 853 clears flag 1803). Insomniac ≈14,4 on 4F; Hypno man ≈6,4 in the roof room.

## Celadon Game Corner: cheaper coins after the Rocket arc

Not a quest, but a money tip. Until the Gloom search ends, coins cost **$20** each (50 for $1,000, 500 for $10,000). From the end of the Gloom search until the Rocket base is cleared, the coin clerk and the free-coins man are gone (the people standing in for them only talk), so you can't buy coins at all. Once the League takes over the Game Corner, coins cost **$5** (50 for $250, 500 for $2,500). If you want Prize Corner items, buy coins afterwards. You still need the gambler's Coin Case ([Celadon City: the stolen Gloom](03-vermilion-to-celadon.md#celadon-city-the-stolen-gloom-story-chain-easy-to-get-stuck)). A laughing man at the slot machines on the left side gives **18 free coins** once, if you have a Coin Case.

*Source:* script file 801 (script 1 L2614/L3041, script 6; $20 while flag 1803 is set, $5 after). The clerk and the 18-coin man (≈5,9) are hidden by flag 1704 from the end of the Gloom search (file 783 @4561) until the Rocket base scene (file 853 @3142).

## Route 16 → Cycling Road → Route 18: the Secret Potion for the Warden's Dragonair

**Where:** the herbal shop's old lady in the Route 16 gatehouse (three steps left of its east door), the Cycling Road (Route 17), and the Route 18 gatehouse at the bottom, where Safari Zone Warden Baoba meets you.

**Who gets it / when:** from the Rainbow Badge ceremony on. Before that she isn't in the gatehouse. You need a **Bicycle**; the gatehouse guard stops you otherwise.

**How it works:**
1. Talk to the old lady: she gives you the **Secret Potion** for a sick Pokémon at the Fuchsia Safari Zone and warns you about "some young people" hogging the road.
2. **Top of Cycling Road:** a Kanto Speed Alliance biker blocks the road. Without the potion he just sends you back. With it: "Force your way through?" → **Yes** and beat **Biker Eddie** (Gastrodon Lv. 42, Piloswine Lv. 43, Skuntank Lv. 44). No pushes you back; ask again.
3. **Riding down, avoid the bikers.** The 12 road bikers are ordinary sight trainers, but each win costs time: **beating two or more of them means the potion arrives too late.** Beating one is fine.
4. **Route 18, at the bottom of Cycling Road (west of the gatehouse):** the gang boss, **Roughneck Paxton**, stops you and battles you whatever you answer. Beat him (losing whites you out and the scene replays).
5. Walk into the Route 18 gatehouse: Baoba takes the potion and hurries back to his Dragonair.

**Outcome** (decided when Baoba takes the potion):

| | Dragonair saved | Dragonair dies |
|---|---|---|
| Condition | you beat Paxton and **at most one** road biker | you beat 2+ road bikers (or somehow reach Baoba without beating Paxton) |
| Baoba (Safari Zone entrance) | thanks you, registers his number, offers **Dratini, Lv. 15, with Extreme Speed** (needs party room; say No and ask again later) | grieves, registers his number; no gift |
| Herbal shop (her house on Route 16 East) | Energy Powder $400, Energy Root $2,000, Heal Powder $400, Revival Herb $5,000 | $800, $3,500, $600, $10,000 |

**Notes:** one chance: the herbalist is hidden after the delivery, so there's no second potion. Either way Baoba then starts the Safari Zone tests (catch a Geodude, then a Sandshrew with the Area Customizer) that make you Safari Zone Owner. The Fuchsia Poké Mart clerk comments on the outcome. Joining or clearing the bike gang is the next entry.

*Source:* script files 207 (script 5), 209 (script 3, L1708–L2104), 210 (script 3, L733, L1368–L1397), 211 (script 5, L501–L1468), 119 (L294, L1583), 206 (script 1, L114), 805 (L82), 853 L3774. The herbalist (≈7,7 in the Route 16 gatehouse) is hidden by flag 1800, which the Rainbow Badge ceremony clears; the outcome is flag 1801 (set = Dragonair dies). File 211 L501–L1468 counts the 12 biker trainer flags 1674–1682, 1687, 1692, 1693 (trainers 314–322, 327, 332, 333); it also checks flag 1689 (trainer 329, Swimmer Marina on Route 41), so beating her before the delivery would also doom Dragonair (suspected hack slip, see known-issues).

## Cycling Road: join the Kanto Speed Alliance or drive it out

**Where:** Roughneck Paxton on Route 18, at the bottom of Cycling Road west of the gatehouse, after you have handed over the Secret Potion.

**How it works:** he asks you to join. The menus:

| Menu | Join | Refuse |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Sure! I've wanted to join!" | "Stop hogging Cycling Road!" |
| 2 | "Long live freedom! I'm in!" | "Freedom isn't anything goes" |
| 3 | "True, rules aren't freedom" | "Then I'm free to stop you!" |

- **Join** (any join option): all 12 Cycling Road bikers count as beaten and never battle you again. The gang stays on the road.
- **Refuse three times:** "If you can beat every member of the Kanto Speed Alliance, we'll leave the Cycling Road for good." Beat all 12 bikers on Route 17 (Hugh, Ethan, Bert, Muramasa, Yancy, Lucky, Stu, Jim, Elliot, Ryder, Wilton, Ryuji), then talk to Paxton again. He battles you once more, this time as a **Double Battle** against his team, sent out two at a time (Magmar, Sandslash, Drapion, Rhyperior, all Lv. 45). Losing whites you out. Win and the gang leaves.

**Reward for driving them out:**
- A kid on **Route 16**, a few steps west of the Route 16 gatehouse's west door, gives **10 Quick Balls**.
- A man on **Route 18**, a few steps west of the Route 18 gatehouse, teaches **Air Slash** for free (repeatable).
- Cycling Road fills with ordinary cyclists.

**Notes:** joining isn't permanent. After the **Marsh Badge**, the Saffron Gym scene clears the gang out anyway, so the Quick Balls and the Air Slash tutor appear then. The **Shiny Stone** item ball near the bottom of Cycling Road (just above the bikers' roadblock) **can't be picked up**: it vanishes during the Mt. Moon roadblock scene early in the game and never comes back, whichever way you deal with the gang (suspected hack bug).

## Fuchsia Gym: the fake Kogas and the ninja bell (the Soul Badge trial)

**Where:** Fuchsia Gym (south-west corner of town, left of the Pokémon Center), then the Fuchsia Forest preserve (the gate in the north-east corner of town, next to the "no entry without Gym Leader Koga's permission" sign).

**How it works:**
1. On your way in, a cutscene by the Gym shows Granny Kin and a henchman talking about how she has bought off Koga, the Gym Leader and head of Kanto's ninja. Koga himself isn't in the scene, and the real Koga is hidden for now.
2. Inside, four fake Kogas turn out to be disguised ninjas when you talk to them (optional battles). The "Koga" standing eight steps straight north of the entrance is Janine in disguise: beat her, and the real Koga appears.
3. Koga won't explain the challenge until your two friends are there. Fetch them: **Green (male player) / Red (female player)** standing outside in the north-east part of town, five steps west of the Shard trader, and **Blue in the Pokémon Center**. Each heads to the Gym once you've talked to them.
4. With all three in the Gym, step up to Koga: the challenge is to bring back the **ninja bell** from the forest northeast of town. The preserve guard now lets you in.
5. In the forest Blue and your friend (Green, or Red for female players) each take a path. Those two paths are blocked for you ("…already took this path"), so take the remaining one. You find your friend being mobbed by Fearow and fight the **Mean Birds**. Your friend then leads you to the bell, which Blue has already picked up.
6. Blue offers a battle for the bell. You can't refuse (No just repeats the question). Beat **Blue**.
7. Koga appears and makes you battle him at once with your tired team: a single **Venonat, Lv. 80** (answering "No" changes his line, not the battle). A cutscene follows: Granny Kin reveals she paid Koga to get rid of you, and Rocket Executives Ariana, Archie and Maxie turn up. Koga refuses to hand you over and calls you to help. You then fight a **single battle against Executive Maxie** (Ninetales, Camerupt, Torkoal, Blaziken, Magmortar, Magcargo, all Lv. 50). Losing this one doesn't white you out; the story continues either way. Rangers arrive and you're taken back to town.
8. Talk to Koga in the Gym: battle **Koga**.

**Reward:** Soul Badge, **TM36 Sludge Bomb**, Koga's Pokégear number.

**Notes:** every loss except the one against Maxie whites you out. Each battle can be retried: walk back into the forest to the same spot and the scene replays from the start of that part. After the badge, Janine offers Attack EV training for a Rawst Berry (see [Gym sparring partners](02-pewter-to-vermilion.md#gym-sparring-partners-ev-training-for-a-berry)). **HM03 Surf** lies on the ground in the forest, about ten steps north of the forest entrance and a little to the east. Two other forest items, a Toxic Orb (west of the entrance area) and a Toxic Plate (in the north-west part of the forest), plus Koga's three disciples only appear once the bell story is over.

*Source:* file 804 (script 11, script 12/L2075; opening cutscene lines 504#16–19), file 807 (script 5/L303), file 806 (scripts 2, 9, 14; L2097, L1931), file 61 (L2224–L7367; Mean Birds trainer 331 @2520, Blue 285 @3815, Koga 286 @4863, Maxie 981 @6373 as `TrainerBattle 981 0 1 0` with no win check). Final Gym battle: trainer 257. Positions: Gym door ≈1195,439, preserve gate ≈1234,403, Green/Red ≈1225,414, Janine-as-Koga ≈7,17 in the Gym; forest items Surf ≈60,53, Toxic Orb ≈41,50, Toxic Plate ≈33,25 (forest entrance ≈52,63). Retries: the forest scenes are step triggers that only switch off after a win (vars 0x40d8/0x40d9 for the Mean Birds and the Blue/Koga/Maxie scene, set at L2690 and L7345).

**Where:** Fuchsia Forest, in the north-east part near the bell clearing (after the bell story).

| Disciple | What he offers |

**Where:** starts from Misty's date menu in the Cerulean Gym, once you're dating her. You're taken to the Resort Zone.

**How it works:** a Pal Park catching scene (no input needed), then Misty signs you both up for the Couples Double Battle. Win **four Multi Battles** in a row with Misty as your partner. A blessing ceremony follows and you return to Cerulean City.

**Notes:** starting the date removes Misty from the Cerulean Gym.
- **If you lose a Couples battle**, you white out and Misty is not in the Gym. Find her at the photo spot at the tip of Cerulean Cape: say Yes to her photo (you need room in your album) and the Cape date plays again. Afterwards she's back in the Gym and you can ask for the Resort Zone date again.
- **If you win**, the date ends in the Gym, but Misty doesn't reappear there or at the Cape, so her next date ("I want you to meet my mom", in Pallet Town) can't be started. The other scenes that send her back to the Gym all belong to the main story, which is over by then, so nothing found brings her back. Save before the date. This looks like a hack bug (tested in an emulator: after the date's ending Misty is neither in the Gym, also after leaving and coming back, nor at the Cape; see [the known issue](known-issues.md#misty-missing-from-cerulean-gym)).

## Resort Zone: the Pal Park's Fixed Catch mode (wild Pokémon by weekday)

**Where:** the Pal Park reception building in the Resort Zone (Route 48, open after the Cianwood Gym story).

**How it works:** talk to the receptionist and ask to take part. You need room for six more Pokémon in your PC Boxes. If you haven't brought Pokémon over from a Game Boy Advance game, she offers **Fixed Catch mode**: pay **$10,000** and you're taken into the park, where you catch with your own Poké Balls. (She says each catch costs $10,000, but the game only charges once, when you go in.)

**What you'll find:**
- **Wild Pokémon:** a different table on each day of the week (by the DS clock). Each day's table is on the [Pal Park location page](/locations/safari-zone-gate/#wild) (listed as Safari Zone Gate, the map's name in the game). Friday's is the only place to find **Stunfisk** (1%). On Sundays you can also surf and fish in the park's pond and sea.
- **Pokémon standing in the park** (Lv. 10, one battle each): two of the eight groups below appear, depending on the day.

- **A:** Treecko, Ralts, Mawile, Carvanha, Seviper, Tropius
- **B:** Torchic, Shroomish, Meditite, Numel, Corphish, Absol
- **C:** Mudkip, Makuhita, Electrike, Spoink, Baltoy, Relicanth
- **D:** Sableye, Gulpin, Swablu, Duskull, Bagon, Beldum
- **E:** Turtwig, Bidoof, Buizel, Buneary, Spiritomb, Carnivine
- **F:** Chimchar, Shinx, Cherubi, Stunky, Riolu, Finneon
- **G:** Piplup, Budew, Shellos, Bronzor, Hippopotas, Snover
- **H:** Pachirisu, Drifloon, Chatot, Gible, Croagunk, Rotom

| Day | Groups |
|---|---|
| Sunday | C and D |
| Monday | A and D from 7:00 to 18:59, E and H the rest of the day |
| Tuesday | E and F |
| Wednesday | A and B |
| Thursday | F and G |
| Friday | B and C |
| Saturday | G and H |

**Notes:** Sunday's table is the same as Cerulean Cave's, probably by mistake in the hack. Monday's groups depend on the hour of the DS clock. The weekday tables and Monday's hours were checked in an emulator (each day loads the table listed; on a Monday the receptionist sets out A and D from 7:00 to 18:59), and wild Pokémon met in the park on a Friday, a Sunday and a Monday all came from that day's table; the rest is read from the game's scripts.

*Source:* file 809 (receptionist: L5439 room check, L5785 weekday branches clearing hide flags 2124–2131, L6431–L6528 $10,000 and warp to map 109); file 12 (48 one-time battles, scripts 7–54); wild table record = 142 + weekday − 1 (arm9 0x0203A7B0, D-1484). Monday branch L6143: `ScrCmd_522` (the current hour, observed) 7–18 → L6679 clears 2124 + 2127 (A, D), else 2128 + 2131 (E, H); emulator `emu_harness.py guide0107 --case monday` (00:30, 06:30 → E+H; 07:30, 12:30, 18:30 → A+D; 19:30, 23:30 → E+H).

## Saffron City (takeover): getting past the barrier into the city

**Where:** the four roads into Saffron City, just outside each gatehouse. Gym Leaders, Elite Four members and friends stand at every one, all stuck behind the same invisible barrier.

**Who gets it / when:** everyone. This is the main story, right after Sabrina's call in Pokémon Tower. Players get stuck here because talking to the Leaders seems to do nothing. The step that's easy to miss is going back to Sabrina at the end.

**How it works:**
1. **East gate (Route 8, the Lavender side):** Sabrina waits here with Agatha and Green (boys) or Red (girls). She explains the barrier and asks you to check the other three entrances while she thinks of another way in.
2. **Check the other three gates.** At each one, talk to at least one of the **people** (talking to their Pokémon doesn't count). The order doesn't matter.

   | Gate | Talk to any of |
   |---|---|
   | North (Route 5, the Cerulean side) | Brock, Misty, Bruno, Bill or Blue |
   | South (Route 6, the Vermilion side) | Lt. Surge, Blaine, Lorelei or Cynthia |
   | West (Route 7, the Celadon side) | Koga, Erika, Lance or Steven |

3. **Go back to Sabrina on Route 8.** She says "So you've checked all of Saffron City's other entrances..." and offers to send you in through her other dimension. Answer **Yes**. If you answer No, talk to her again and she asks again; nothing is lost. If she still repeats her first speech, a gate is missing.
4. You land in the **pocket dimension**, the copy of Saffron from [Sabrina's willpower test](05-saffron-cinnabar.md#saffron-gym-sabrinas-willpower-test-and-the-pocket-dimension-before-the-takeover). Go to the house in the south-east, where Mr. Psychic's house stands in the real city, and talk to the caretaker at the back. She gives you the **Sub. Doll** and sends you into the occupied Saffron Gym.
5. In Saffron, Blue turns up (Bill sent him in through the PC storage system) and the two of you fight off the Rockets together. Blue then suggests sneaking into **Silph Co.**, where their boss and the key to the barrier should be.

**Next:** Silph Co. In Rotom's Room, a glowing floor panel leads on to the hostages' floor ([Rotom's Room](05-saffron-cinnabar.md#rotoms-room-silph-co-change-rotoms-form), [the employee's Lapras](#silph-co-saffron-takeover-the-employees-lapras-missable)). Then go up to Ariana and the [president's office](#silph-co-presidents-office-the-goodshow-meeting-and-the-saffron-takeover-finale). Optional on the way: [help Ma Baoguo](05-saffron-cinnabar.md#saffron-city-takeover-help-ma-baoguo-fight-off-team-rocket) outside the Hunyuan Dojo.

**Notes:** Fly can't get you over the barrier either: Lance says it covers the sky. Read from the game's scripts; the Blue scene in step 5 was also played in an emulator.

## Silph Co. president's office: the Goodshow meeting and the Saffron takeover finale

**Where:** the president's office at the top of Silph Co. Two scenes play here:
1. **Before the takeover:** Lance brings you, Blue and Green/Red to meet President Goodshow. He gives you **3 Rare Candies**. The two answers to his question only change one line. This meeting makes the Pokémon Tower ghosts appear for Sabrina's trial ([Pokémon Tower: make Sabrina laugh](03-vermilion-to-celadon.md#pokémon-tower-make-sabrina-laugh-the-marsh-badge-trial)).
2. **End of the takeover:** the only way in is the door behind **Ariana** on the Silph Co. top floor. Beat her (losing whites you out) and you walk straight into the office, where the Rocket Boss is pressing the president to join Team Rocket.
**How it works:**
1. The game asks "Team Rocket's boss seems to be talking with Silph's president. Act now?": **Listen a little longer** / **Attack the barrier Pokémon** / **Attack the Rocket Boss directly**. Listening lets you overhear the president hand over Shota's Master Ball, then asks again (barrier or boss only). The choice changes only a few lines: every branch ends the same way.
2. The boss spots you and his Nidoqueen floors you. Your **Sub. Doll** (from the Saffron Gym caretaker, [Saffron Gym: Sabrina's willpower test](05-saffron-cinnabar.md#saffron-gym-sabrinas-willpower-test-and-the-pocket-dimension-before-the-takeover)) takes the final blow, and your Pokémon knocks out the Hypno that powers the barrier.
3. Battle **Rocket Boss** (Nidoqueen, Beedrill, Rhydon, Marowak, Swampert, Sandslash, all Lv. 100). **Losing doesn't white out** and the story goes on either way. If you win, Ariana reports the barrier is cracking and the Rockets flee by train; if you lose, Blue tells you they retreated once the barrier broke.
4. You wake up in the Pokémon Center with your friends. Lance gives you a **Vs. Recorder**, the Sub. Doll is used up, and Saffron is freed.
**Reward:** Vs. Recorder (plus 3 Rare Candies from the earlier meeting).

*Source:* script file 834 (script 30 Ariana, L1401, Warp to map 377 then L3302; choice L3400/L3756; battle L4755; Vs. Recorder L5641; end flags L5948–L6010), script file 795 (script 1, coord var 0x40c5).
