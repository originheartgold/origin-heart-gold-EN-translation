## Route 46: Team Rocket's dig and Crystal taken hostage (story; romance choice; continues Ilex Forest and Goldenrod City's romance entry)

**Where:** the north end of Route 46. The scene starts as you walk up to the Dark Cave entrance, two steps south of its door. You can come up from the Route 29 gatehouse or out of Dark Cave.

**Who gets it / when:** early in the Johto chapter, right after Gold and Crystal argue at the Route 31 Dark Cave entrance ([Route 31: give Crystal your Flash TM](08-cherrygrove-to-azalea.md#route-31-give-crystal-your-flash-tm-the-way-into-dark-cave)). The scene is **required**: the Sprout Tower memorial ceremony in Violet City only starts once it has played.

**How it works:**
1. Crystal has cornered Cassidy and Butch, who are digging. They recite their motto, then you step in: **Multi Battle** with Crystal as your partner against Rocket Elites Cassidy and Butch (Lv. 67–69, three Pokémon each). Crystal's team is weak (Chikorita Lv. 10, Parasect Lv. 24, Cubone, Natu Lv. 9), so expect to carry the battle.
2. Executives **Charon and Lawrence** arrive. You refuse Crystal's help and face them in a **Double Battle** (six Pokémon each, Lv. 87–88). There is **no healing** between step 1 and step 2. Losing either battle whites you out, and the scene replays when you walk back in.
3. Charon's Dugtrio pops out of the ground and grabs Crystal. Gold turns up. Charon orders you both to put your Pokémon away. "Do as Charon says and put your Pokémon away?"
   - **Yes:** you comply.
   - **No:** you threaten them instead, and Gold complies for you. **This locks Crystal out of the romance route** (male players; see [the romance route's central entry](09-ilex-goldenrod.md#romance-route-how-its-unlocked-how-your-partner-is-chosen-and-what-locks-a-partner-out-central-entry)).
   The scene continues the same way either way: Gold's "cue shot" Poké Balls free Crystal, Charon uses Arena Trap to escape, and Gold and Crystal start a Pokédex race.
**Reward:** story only. The dig site clears, and the Stantler herd blocking Route 30 leaves ([Cherrygrove City to Azalea Town](08-cherrygrove-to-azalea.md)).

**Notes:**
- **Answer Yes** if you want Crystal as a partner. If you answered No, you can still undo it later: give Crystal your Super Rod in Olivine City before the Mineral Badge ([Olivine City: give Crystal your Super Rod](10-ecruteak-olivine.md#olivine-city-give-crystal-your-super-rod-before-the-mineral-badge-romance)), or make the "more attractive" wish at the Six Island pond before the Dream World ([Island Forest (Six Island)](05-saffron-cinnabar.md#island-forest-six-island-before-the-lucky-meowth-god)).
- **Missable shop:** the man a short walk north-west of the Route 29 gatehouse (about 14 steps north and 8 west of its door) sells items that "roll down" from the dig: Metronome $2,000, Iron Ball $3,000, Smooth Rock $5,000, Metal Powder $2,000. He stops selling for good once this scene has played ("Since the digging up there stopped, I haven't picked up a single thing").

*Source:* script file 259 (coord script 4 on var 0x40ac = 1; L227–L1789 battles; L2133 Yes, L2267 No sets flag 2145; L3570–L3594 sets flags 1807 and 1808 and var 0x40ac = 2; script 1 shop, flag 1808), file 230 L594 (Route 31 sets 0x40ac = 1), file 18 (Sprout Tower 3F coord needs 0x40ac = 2). Positions: trigger row ≈627–629,331, Dark Cave door 628,329, shop man ≈618,366.

## Route 43: a Chesto Berry for the listless Sentret (→ 2 Lum Berries; before the Rocket HQ is cleared)

**Where:** Route 43, the girl with a Sentret beside the sign just north of the gatehouse at the south end of the route (she's on the sign's west side). After the Mahogany Rocket HQ chapter she stands on the other side of the sign, a couple of steps east of it.

**Who gets it / when:** from your first visit until you clear the Team Rocket HQ under Mahogany. **Missable:** once the HQ is cleared, she no longer asks for the berry.

**How it works:**
1. Talk to her. She asks for a Chesto Berry → **Yes** with one in your Bag. She takes it and feeds it to Sentret. It doesn't help ("I should take Sentret to the Pokémon Center"). The real cause is Team Rocket's radio waves.
2. Clear the Team Rocket HQ, then talk to her again.
**Reward:** **2 Lum Berries** (once).

**Notes:** without a Chesto Berry she just says you don't have one. You can come back with one. If you never gave her the berry, after the HQ she only says Sentret has recovered.

*Source:* script file 254 (script 7, L776–L1225; flag 507 set at new game, cleared when you give the berry, set again with the reward; flag 202 set at the end of the HQ chapter, file 90 L2861). Positions: girl ≈530,147, after the HQ ≈534,146.

## Lake of Rage: the red Gyarados and the radio transmitters (story; how to move on)

**Where:** Lake of Rage, straight after the Route 42 Entei scene and Mt. Mortar.

**How it works:**
1. Walk north from Route 43 to the south shore. Lance stands near the water with his Dragonite. **Talk to him**: the scene doesn't start on its own.
2. A Gyarados attacks a tourist. You fight a **wild red Gyarados (Lv. 30)**. It's a normal wild battle, so you can catch it. Knocking it out or running away also moves the scene on. Losing whites you out.
3. More Gyarados follow. A Magikarp suddenly evolves into a red Gyarados; Lance's Dragonite stuns it and Lance catches it. A third one attacks, Pryce's Mamoswine holds it back with an ice wall, and Gold catches that one.
4. Pryce says his radio is being jammed. Everyone splits up to look for a transmitter. There are three people in lab coats with a boxy radio beside them. Gold takes the one on the west shore and Lance the one on the north-east shore. **Yours is the "researcher" on the north shore, about 9 steps east and 8 steps north of the door of the house in the north-west corner.**
5. Talk to him and pick **"Neat gadget there"** ("Seen a transmitter?" goes nowhere). At "Investigate the device by force?" answer **Yes**. You fight **Scientist Yamada** (Manectric, Drapion, Porygon2, Vileplume, Lv. 62–65). He then escapes south on a Sharpedo. Answering No just ends the talk. Ask again as often as you like.
6. Go back to Pryce and tell him. He waits with his Mamoswine on the south shore, just south-west of the sign by the water. He concludes the real source is in Mahogany Town. The Team Rocket HQ chapter starts: next, [Mahogany Town → Team Rocket HQ](11-cianwood-mahogany.md#mahogany-town--team-rocket-hq-the-radio-wave-investigation-story-where-to-go-next).
**Reward:** story progress. Also see [Lake of Rage → Route 44: small extras](#lake-of-rage--route-44-small-extras) (the Route 43 gatehouse TM).

Script 18 is Lance's talk script (object ≈533,79); the wild battle is at L2247 (`CheckBattleWon` L2254: only a loss goes to the white-out at L5589, so a flee continues like a win), Lance catches the evolved Magikarp (msgs 14–16, 36), Gold catches the third (msg 26); L4167–L4203 send Lance to ≈541,37 and Gold to ≈490,73. ## Team Rocket HQ B3F: the two passwords and the Secret Key door (continues Battle Frontier, Cianwood City and Mahogany Town's "Mahogany Town → Team Rocket HQ")

**Where:** Team Rocket HQ B3F, reached by the stairs from B2F. The Executives' room door is in the middle of the floor. Behind it is the way down to Archer's room.

**Who gets it / when:** during the HQ chapter, after you pick up the **Secret Key** on B2F ([Mahogany Town → Team Rocket HQ](11-cianwood-mahogany.md#mahogany-town--team-rocket-hq-the-radio-wave-investigation-story-where-to-go-next), step 3).

**How it works:** the door takes the Secret Key **and two passwords**. You get them from two separate scenes, in either order:

| Password | Where | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **"Slowpoke Tail"** | West room. Walking in triggers the scene: Cyrus and Charon have just knocked out Gold's Typhlosion | Stop them. You fight **Charon and Cyrus in a 2-on-1 Double Battle** (Lv. 87–90: Charon 87–88, Cyrus 89–90; six Pokémon each). Afterwards Gold tells you the password. |
| **"Raticate Tail"** | East room. Walking in makes Ariana and Lawrence corner Crystal | Crystal joins you for a **Multi Battle vs Ariana and Lawrence** (Lv. 87–90). Crystal then says the password is on the room's PC. Talk to the **Scientist guarding the PC** at the north end of the room and beat **Scientist Watanabe** (Swellow, Hypno, Machamp, Lucario, Lv. 62–65). He gives up the password. |

1. With both passwords, examine the door. It says "You entered both passwords" and opens. With only one, it says "You entered one password, but there are two!" and stays shut. Without the Secret Key: "It needs a key to open."
2. Behind the door, "Giovanni" is waiting. He's Petrel in disguise. You fight **Executive Petrel** (Nidoking, Exploud, Slaking, Toxicroak, Skuntank, Muk, Lv. 86–87).
3. Walk north past him to the top of the room, which takes you to Archer's room ([Mahogany Town → Team Rocket HQ](11-cianwood-mahogany.md#mahogany-town--team-rocket-hq-the-radio-wave-investigation-story-where-to-go-next), step 5).

**Notes:**
- Losing any of these battles whites you out. None of them heals you, and some come back to back, so bring healing items.
- Lance's fight against Maxie and Archie in the north-east corner is only a scene. He tells you to go on.
- Crystal's hint names only the PC. The Scientist battles you when you talk to him. Talking to him again repeats the password.

*Source:* script file 91 (script 8 → L1983–L2262, `TrainerBattle 553/817`, flag 502; script 1 → L2290–L2570, `MultiBattle 911/893/549`, flag 503; script 3, `TrainerBattle 912`, flag 211; script 4 → L2589–L3030, `HasItem 467`, `TrainerBattle 816`, flag 640; script 2 warp to map 133). Positions: Executives' door ≈23,16; west-room trigger ≈20,24; east-room trigger ≈38,23; PC Scientist ≈42,15; warp ≈19,3. Party levels from trainers 553 (Charon, Lv. 87–88) and 817 (Cyrus, Lv. 89–90). Reachability (review 2026-10-06): the west-room trigger needs var 0x40B2 = 2 (set on B1F/B2F, files 89/90); the east-room trigger needs var 0x40B5 = 1, which only the Lavender Town Blue battle sets (file 17 L3796, a forced trigger that replays until won, per guide 03); the later values 3–11 come from post-HQ romance dates. ## Route 43: break up the Pidgeot and Noctowl flock (→ Sharp Beak; after the Rocket HQ)

**Where:** Route 43, a flock of three Pidgeot and three Noctowl squabbling in the grass in the middle of the route. A man about 17 steps north of them explains that Pidgeot moved in after the radio-wave incident and started fighting the Noctowl.

**Who gets it / when:** after the Mahogany Team Rocket HQ chapter (the flock appears then).

**How it works:**
1. Your party must be **exactly two Pokémon: a Pidgeot and a Noctowl**. With anything else (a missing species, or extra Pokémon) you shout at them and "They're in no mood to pay you any attention."
2. Examine the flock → "Try to break it up?" → **Yes**. You show that your own Pidgeot and Noctowl get along. The flock refuses, so you take them all on in a **Double Battle** against two "Wild Birds" Trainers with six Lv. 50 Pidgeot and Noctowl each (**12 birds in total**). It counts as a Trainer battle, so you can't catch them.
3. Win, and the flock flies off. Something drops where they stood.
**Reward:** pick up the **Sharp Beak** (once).

**Notes:** losing whites you out, and you can try again. After this the man north of the flock talks about the Lake of Rage's Magikarp.

*Source:* script file 254 (script 18, L1169–L4176: `PlayerHasSpecies 18` and `164`, `GetPartyCount = 2`, `TrainerBattle 919, 919`; script 19 gives item 244, flag 511; flag 589 cleared by file 90 L2857). Trainer 919 has six Lv. 50 birds. Positions: flock ≈529–530,122–124, man ≈534,105.

## Route 44 → Ice Path: the Cleffa con man (→ Dawn Stone)

**Where:** Route 44, the girl with the Cleffa at the west end, a few steps north-east of the sign at the route's west entrance. The con men wait on Ice Path 1F, in the middle of the floor.

**Who gets it / when:** any time you pass Route 44 on the way to the Ice Path.

**How it works:**
1. Talk to the girl. A man who calls himself a Mossdeep Space Center researcher talks her into giving him her Cleffa "to send it home to the moon", and walks off with it.
2. On Ice Path 1F you find him with a partner, bragging that Cleffa family Pokémon pick up Moon Stones. "Help the girl get Cleffa back?" → **Yes**.
3. You fight **Scientist Zenith and Shady Guy Watanabe together** (2-on-1): Muk, Dusknoir, Wobbuffet, Crawdaunt / Flygon, Scyther, Exploud, Carnivine, Lv. 62–65. Losing whites you out; the pair stay put and you can try again.
4. You take Cleffa, and the game takes you straight back to the girl on Route 44.
**Reward:** **Dawn Stone** ("the most precious thing I have").

**Notes:** "No" at step 2 does nothing; talk to them again. One-time. Once it's done the con men are gone.

*Source:* script file 99 (Ice Path 1F, script 11, L1829–L3503; flags 594, 203, 199); file 257 (Route 44, script 4, L108–L749 sets 199 and clears 594). Positions: girl ≈555,172; con men ≈31–32,26–27.

## Route 44: the kid with his brother's Heracross (→ Never-Melt Ice)

**Where:** Route 44. A boy and a Sneasel kid are arguing in the middle of the route, on its south side. The boy's older brother stands at the east end, not far west of the Ice Path entrance.

**Who gets it / when:** any time.

**How it works:**
1. Talk to either kid. The boy's Heracross flattens the Sneasel, then he asks if you want "a taste of Heracross's power". Answer **Yes**. If you say No he asks again, and you can say Yes then. You fight **Bug Catcher Nate** (a Lv. 82 Heracross plus three Lv. 7 cocoons). When he loses he blurts out that it's his big brother's Heracross, and he runs off to his brother.
2. Before this, the brother only says his little brother keeps borrowing his Pokémon. Now talk to the brother and accept his battle: **Ace Trainer Burke** (Politoed, Pinsir, Heracross, Scizor, Armaldo, Butterfree, Lv. 81–82). If you say No to him, he just ends the talk; talk to him again.
3. He recognises you as the Champion and asks what you thought of his brother. Both answers ("He used Heracross well" / "Never saw his Pokémon") lead to the same talk about growing up with your own Pokémon.
**Reward:** **Never-Melt Ice** (once).

**Notes:** losing either battle whites you out. You can retry.

*Source:* script file 257 (scripts 6/7 → L2092–L3388, `TrainerBattle 920`, flag 2239, clears 488; script 10 → L2419, L3390–L4112, `TrainerBattle 921`, item 246, flag 204). Trainer 921 has six Pokémon (Butterfree Lv. 81 included). Positions: kids ≈591–594,186; brother ≈619,174.

## Ice Path 1F: the Icy Rock duel (→ Icy Rock)

**Where:** Ice Path 1F, north part of the floor. Two Trainers face off with a Glalie and an Abomasnow. The Icy Rock sits "on the other side of the ice", about 14 steps east of them.

**Who gets it / when:** any time.

**How it works:**
1. Walk up to either Trainer. They play out their "third duel today" and tie again. "Talk to them?" → **Battling over who gets an item?**
2. → **I want the Icy Rock too.** They agree on a new rule: beat both of them **back to back** and the rock is yours. "Want in?" → Yes.
3. Battle **Ace Trainer Sue** (Glalie, Piloswine, Aron Lv. 1, Lucario, Swampert; Lv. 81–82) and **Veteran Frost** (Abomasnow, Jynx, Castform, Walrein, Mamoswine; Lv. 81–82), one after the other with **no healing in between**. Which of them goes first depends on where you stand.
4. Win both and pick up the **Icy Rock** item ball.
**Reward:** **Icy Rock**. Afterwards Frost stands on the east side of the floor, about 14 steps north and 9 west of the exit to Blackthorn City, and offers a **repeatable rematch**, Single or Double Battle (Double needs two Pokémon).

**Notes:** these are Lv. 81–82 teams, far above the route's other Trainers. Losing either battle whites you out, but nothing is lost: talk to them again to retry. "None of my business" or "Never mind" just ends the talk.

*Source:* script file 99 (scripts 3, 5, 8–10; L1369–L4544; flags 2244, 504, 2245). Positions: duelists ≈33,10–13; Icy Rock ≈47,10; Frost's rematch spot ≈46,26.

## Ice Path B2F: Nurse Joy's Suicune trap (story)

**Where:** Ice Path B2F, from the ledge where you land in the middle of the floor to the south-west corner.

**Who gets it / when:** the story's Ice Path crossing, with Crystal (she studies the "damp soil" Suicune leaves, on 1F and B2F).

**How it works:** you meet Eusine chasing Suicune, and follow the trail to it. The "Joy from Blackthorn City" claims Suicune is hurt and sets up a "treatment device"; it's the Team Rocket Joy, and the device is a brainwashing trap. Then:
1. Two battles in a row against **Team Rocket Grunts** (Mightyena, Weezing, Magcargo, Honchkrow, Lv. 62–65). Joy heals her Grunts in between.
2. Goh bursts in and catches Suicune, but it won't obey him.
3. Battle **Rocket Elite Joy** (Chansey, Seviper, Drapion, Granbull, Mismagius, Persian, Lv. 86–87). Losing any of these battles whites you out and the scene replays.
4. Crystal's Meganium destroys the device and calms Suicune with Aromatherapy; Suicune runs off, Team Rocket retreats and Goh leaves (he later hangs around Ice Path 1F).
**Reward:** none (story).

*Source:* script file 101 (scripts 3 and 4; coord triggers at 49,32 and 13,55 on var 0x40a2 = 1 and 2; sets 0x40a2 = 3, flags 2242, 2243).

## Blackthorn City → Route 45: the stolen Badges (→ 3 Energy Powder)

**Where:** the Trainer in Blackthorn City, three steps south of the Pokémon Center door; the "Senior Trainer" on Route 45, a few steps south-west of the Dark Cave exit, with two kids listening to him.

**Who gets it / when:** talk to the Blackthorn Trainer first. He has lost his seven Johto Badges. Until you do, the Senior Trainer on Route 45 only brags about his "8 Badges".

**How it works:**
1. On Route 45, the Senior Trainer brags about his seven Johto Badges plus a Cerulean Badge, and the kids are about to pay him for lessons. Talk to him and choose **"Earned those Badges?"**, then **"Tell me your Badge story"**. ("Someone lost their Badges" only makes him threaten to report you.)
2. He offers four stories. Only one gives him away:

| Question | Result |
|---|---|

**Who gets it / when:** any time after the Team Rocket HQ chapter. You **need** the spray in the final chapter: Giovanni and Cyrus go through this tunnel, and the kid turns you back at the entrance unless you carry an Anti-Age Spray.

**How it works:**
1. **Swinub Trainer.** His Swinub ran into the tunnel and came out old and frail. He's waiting for Durin Berries from Mahogany ([Mahogany Pokémon Center: Durin Berries for the Ice Path](11-cianwood-mahogany.md#mahogany-pokémon-center-durin-berries-for-the-ice-path--15-durin-berries)). Talk to him with **at least 10 Durin Berries**: he takes 10 and gives you a **Sacred Ash**. With fewer than 10 he turns you away.
2. **Old man (west).** Before step 1 he only warns you about the curse. After it, choose **"How do I get through?"**. He explains the spray and gives the recipe.
3. Bring him **10 Durin Berries, 5 Rare Candies, 1 Sacred Ash, 1 Fresh Water and $10,000**, then choose **"Make me an Anti-Age Spray"**. He takes it all and gives you the **Anti-Age Spray**. Missing anything: "You don't have enough ingredients" (or not enough money); nothing is taken.
4. **At the tunnel.** The kid stops you ("Hey! Wait up!"). If you carry the spray, you use it and may go in. Without it you're turned back.
**Reward:** access to the tunnel and the statue at its end (see below). The Swinub Trainer's Sacred Ash.

**Notes:**
- **You need 20 Durin Berries in total** (10 for Swinub, 10 for the spray). The Mahogany nurse gives 15 and Fuchsia City gives 1, so you'll have to grow more in Berry Pots or soft soil (not confirmed in game; see [the known issue](known-issues.md#durin-berries-for-the-anti-age-spray)). Sacred Ash also comes from item balls and a Vermilion trade, so you can use the Swinub one or another.
- The old man says each bottle covers one trip and that he'll make more. In fact the spray is **never used up**: keep it in the Bag and it works every time. He refuses to make a second while you hold one.
- Until you win the battle in Dialga's space (final chapter), the tunnel re-locks every time you re-enter the floor, so keep the spray. After that battle it stays open. Your final Hall of Fame entry locks it once more; one more use of the spray then opens it for good.
- The kid calls the tunnel cursed when he stops you. Only when you talk to him while it's already open does he say he "can't feel the curse anymore".
- **The final-chapter scene** (east section, a few steps north of the B3F stairs, after the Reversal Cave chapter): Giovanni has Giratina break the curse; the boy who had turned into an old man (a few steps east of the stairs) is young again, and Giovanni and Cyrus walk through the tunnel.
- **The statue at the end:** in the final chapter, after that scene, it "is giving off a mysterious power" and sends you to **Dialga's space**. After your final Hall of Fame it works only if you carry the **Adamant Orb** ("The statue is resonating with the Adamant Orb you carry!"). Any other time it's just "An ancient Pokémon statue."

**Where:** the Mystri Stage, the temple of Arceus. Regigigas stands on the path to the steps, and the stage's centre circle is just above it, at the top of the steps.

**Who gets it / when:** after your final Hall of Fame entry, if you could get back in.
