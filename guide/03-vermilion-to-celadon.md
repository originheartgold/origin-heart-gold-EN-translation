## Vermilion City: the construction-site dispute (choose a side)

Continues in [Vermilion City: after the construction-site clash](02-pewter-to-vermilion.md#vermilion-city-after-the-construction-site-clash).

**Where:** the Vermilion Pokémon Center. From the street scene when you first arrive in Vermilion until the clash, Blue stands with his Wartortle in the middle of the room, about six steps up from the door. Green (male player) or Red (female player) stands with Ivysaur on the left (west) side.

**Who gets it / when:** everyone. It's story, but the answer changes gifts and your romance options.

**How it works:**

1. Talk to either partner. Blue was turned away at the Gym because the Leader has no time; both ask whether you know what's going on in town. Answering Yes before you've gathered the facts gets "Go ask around town some more".
2. Hear out all six people in town (talking to them is enough):

   | Who | Where |
   |---|---|
   | Lt. Surge | at the fenced construction site east of the Pokémon Center |
   | Granny Kin | right next to Surge, on his east side |
   | Resident (Surge applied for a ban 30 days ago) | a few steps south-west of the Poké Mart door |
   | Sailor (the city was built by sailors) | south, near the port: about eight steps north of the Harbor door |
   | Man (Professor Birch warned about a Diglett disaster) | the south-east corner of town |
   | Woman (grew up in Vermilion) | just west of the construction site |

   **Warning:** the next Yes picks your side and locks one partner out of the romance route (see the table below). It can only be undone by the Six Island wish.
3. Go back and answer Yes again. The partner you talk to decides your side:
   - **Blue:** "Are you coming with me?" to help Lt. Surge hold the site entrance against the townsfolk. Yes = **law side**.
   - **Green / Red:** "Will you help me?" to help the townsfolk stop the construction. Yes = **townsfolk side**.
   - Answering No sets nothing; that partner goes it alone ("Tch. I can handle it without you." from Blue, "Looks like it's up to me" from Green/Red). You can ask again.
4. Saying Yes heals your party, ends the day and starts the clash next morning (Lorelei then announces the League's ban). The law side fights two Multi Battles in a row; the townsfolk side fights one, against Lt. Surge and Blue.

**What the choice changes:**

| | Law side (Blue) | Townsfolk side (Green/Red) |
|---|---|---|
| Romance | **Green/Red is locked out as your partner** | **Blue is locked out as your partner** |
| Thank-you gifts | no King's Rock, no Big Mushrooms | **King's Rock** (the sailor near the port) and **2 Big Mushrooms** (the woman west of the site) |

Gifts both sides get ([Vermilion City: after the construction-site clash](02-pewter-to-vermilion.md#vermilion-city-after-the-construction-site-clash)): 2 Cheri Berries, a Magnet, and Lt. Surge's Light Ball (a Thunder Stone for Pikachu starters).

**Notes:** this is the earliest romance lock in the game. If you want Blue later, side with Blue here; if you want Green (male player) or Red (female player), side with them. The lock is not quite final: the one-time Amulet Coin wish in the Island Forest on Six Island ("I want to be more attractive") lifts it again. Lt. Surge's line after the badge does **not** change with your side: everyone hears the townsfolk version. The King's Rock matters for [Cerulean's TM04 trade](02-pewter-to-vermilion.md#cerulean-city-kings-rock-for-tm04).

**If you lose the clash:** you white out and the side choice is reset, so you choose again and can switch sides. The romance lock from your first choice stays, so switching locks out **both** partners (until the Six Island wish). Tested in an emulator up to the first battle: the clash clears your side choice before it starts, and siding with the other partner afterwards keeps the first lock and adds the second. The lost battle itself wasn't played; the white-out is read from the script.

*Source:* file 777 (scripts 2, 11; L376–L677: law side sets flags 1064 and 2141 and clears 1065 at L614; townsfolk side sets 1065 and 2153 and clears 1064 at L651; both set 1058 and var 0x40A4 = 1), file 774 (scripts 1, 12, 13, 21, 23, 24; L2345, L4014, L4089, L4135, L4040, L7768). Partner positions in the Pokémon Center ≈9,13 (Blue) and ≈3,15 (Green/Red), shown until the arrival scene (file 774 script 14, step trigger ≈1297,295 outside the Pokémon Center; first run at L2623 clears hide flag 1058). Town positions: Surge ≈1312,297, Granny Kin ≈1314,297, resident ≈1308,312, sailor ≈1308,332, man ≈1325,333, woman ≈1304,298. The clash clears both 1064 and 1065 (774 L2664/L2668 and L4909/L4913); only the end of the townsfolk route sets 1065 again (774 @9403). ## Vermilion City: the Good Rod (show Ryochi a Horsea)

**Where:** the Fishing Guru's house, the westernmost door on Vermilion's north row (five steps west of the Pokémon Center door). Ryochi, vice-president of the Kanto Fishing Association.

**How it works:**

1. Talk to Ryochi. He asks you to hook a **Horsea** and show it to him. Then "Do you have a Horsea with you?" → Yes → pick it from your party. He only looks; you keep it.
2. Ways to get a Horsea:
   - The wild Horsea in the water on the west side of town, five steps west of the central house's south-east door: feed it 5 Yache Berries and catch it (Lv. 25, [Vermilion City: feed the Wingull and the Horsea](02-pewter-to-vermilion.md#vermilion-city-feed-the-wingull-and-the-horsea)).
   - **Easier (townsfolk side only, after the clash, before you get the Good Rod):** after talking to Ryochi, talk to the angler on the south shore, a few steps south-east of the central house's south-east door. He fishes the shore Horsea up for you, or, if it's already gone, just gives you one: **Horsea, Lv. 25** (needs a free party slot, one time). "Don't tell Ryochi I fished up that Horsea for you." He won't help while you're carrying **Oak's Parcel**.
   - Fishing one up with the Old Rod doesn't work: Horsea never appears on the Old Rod.
3. Show it to Ryochi.

**Reward:** **Good Rod**. After that he only talks about the Lake of Rage angler.

**Notes:** showing the wrong Pokémon gets "That's not a Horsea..."; you can retry freely. The angler only helps after you've spoken to Ryochi.

*Source:* file 779 (script 1; Ryochi clears flag 1419 after the Good Rod, @222), file 774 (script 20; L3932, L7523, L7640). Fishing Guru door ≈1292,294; shore Horsea ≈1305,319; angler ≈1312,321. The angler (774 script 20) needs flag 1056 (clash over), 1065 (townsfolk side), 1419 (Ryochi's request active), no item 484 (Oak's Parcel) in the Bag, and 1867 clear (his one-time gift, set when he gives it). Horsea appears only in Surf, Good Rod and Super Rod slots across all encounter tables, never Old Rod.

## Vermilion City: the Pokémon Fan Club (three members → Bike Voucher)

**Where:** the Pokémon Fan Club, south-west of the Pokémon Center (the westernmost door on the middle row of houses).

**How it works:** the chairman won't reward you until you've won over three members. Do them in any order:

1. **Pokéfan Roxy** (west side, next to her Bayleef): "Want to battle and see?" → Yes. **Bayleef, Delcatty, Nidorina, all Lv. 21.** Losing whites you out; you must win for it to count.
2. **The Clefairy fan** (south-west): wants a Clefairy Poké Doll. Buy a **Poké Doll** (Vermilion Mart, $1,000) and give it to him. He gives you **Leftovers**.
3. **The quiz lady** (north, beside the chairman): eight questions. One wrong answer ends the quiz ("Wrong! Think it over and come back.") and you restart from question 1. Unlimited retries.

   | # | Question | Answer | Position |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | 1 | Which is the smallest? | **Diglett** | 2nd |
   | 2 | Which is the lightest? | **Chingling** | 4th |
   | 3 | First round of a Super Contest judges... | **Condition and coat** | 3rd |
   | 4 | How many rounds in a Super Contest? | **3** | 1st |
   | 5 | Which can't learn Attract? | **Staryu** | 2nd |
   | 6 | What does infatuation do? | **Can't attack 50%** | 4th |
   | 7 | Which does NOT raise friendship? | **Winning battles** | 2nd |
   | 8 | What kind of Pokémon do you like best? | any answer | |

   Reward: **Shed Shell**.

4. Talk to the chairman (north-east). He makes you an Honorary Member and gives you the **Bike Voucher**. Take it to the Cerulean Bike Shop for a **Bicycle**.
5. Talk to him again and say Yes to his story for one more gift: **Thunder Stone** if you chose Charmander, **Rare Candy** otherwise. Needs Bag space.

**Notes:** this is the only Bike Voucher source found in this page. The Bike Shop checks only for the voucher, with no badge check.

*Source:* file 780 (scripts 1–4, 8; flags 1075 Roxy, 1077 Clefairy fan, 1076 quiz, 1078 voucher, 248 extra gift), file 764 (Bike Shop), trainer 521. Fan Club door ≈1293,309.

## Vermilion City: the seaside cook (Shoal Salt + Big Mushroom)

**Where:** the east half of the central house. Use the door directly south of the Poké Mart, across the street (the house's south-east door), not the west door.

**How it works:** the cook wants seasonings for seafood. Bring **1 Shoal Salt** and **1 Big Mushroom** and talk to her; she takes both automatically. If you're missing either, she just repeats the request.
- Shoal Salt: the old street merchant in Vermilion ($2,000; he stands about 16 steps east of the Poké Mart door and is always there; he also sells Deep Sea Tooth and Deep Sea Scale for $2,500 and Soft Sand for $3,000), S.S. Anne shops/gift.
- Big Mushroom: the townsfolk-side gift above (2), Route 9 shop ($3,000), hidden items elsewhere.

**Reward:** **3 Sacred Ash** ("the soot from the bottom of my pot"). One time.

*Source:* file 781 (script 3; flag 1074). Door ≈1310,318. Street merchant: file 774 script 27, ≈1326,310.

## S.S. Anne: Teddiursa's honey and the salt (starts the party)

**Where:** on board the S.S. Anne, after the boarding speech. The girl with the Teddiursa and her grandfather are in the first north-west cabin (1F north-west rooms, the door at the top left of the hall). The cook is in the kitchen on B1F: take the stairs at the top middle of the hall down, and he's a few steps to the right, next to the fridge. The Captain is in the captain's cabin (1F, far east door).

**Who gets it / when:** everyone. You need it to move the story on: the party (and the hijack after it) starts only once you've **given the girl Honey and met the Captain**.

**How it works:**
1. Talk to the girl. Her Teddiursa won't eat the ship's food and she asks you to get **Honey** from the kitchen.
2. The cook on B1F has Honey but has run out of salt. He asks for salt in exchange. (He only asks once the girl has asked you for Honey; before that he just mutters about the salt.)
3. Get one **Shoal Salt**. There are three ways:
   - **Blue** (in the main hall, 1F west) gives you one free, but **only if you're carrying less than $6000 and have no Shoal Salt in your Bag**, and only after the cook has asked for salt. With $6000 or more he just talks about the party. Green/Red only says "ask Blue". Blue's offer uses the same "party not started yet" check as the Captain's HM01: tested in an emulator, both hand over their item in that state and neither does without it. In the same test, boarding from Vermilion Harbor did not put the ship into that state: the Captain said the party had already started and Blue offered a battle instead of the salt. When the game switches to it before the party is still open.
   - The merchant in the north-west rooms (lower-right cabin) sells one for **$6000** ("four times the price"), also only after the cook has asked.
   - Any Honey you already carry also works. The girl only checks that you have Honey, so you can skip the salt step entirely.
4. Give the Shoal Salt to the cook → **Honey**.
5. Give the Honey to the girl ("Give away the Honey?" → Yes).
6. Talk to the Captain in his cabin: he gives you **HM01 (Cut)**.
7. When both 5 and 6 are done, the party is announced.

**Reward:** HM01 from the Captain; **Water Stone** from the grandfather.

**The Water Stone is missable.** If you meet the Captain *first* and then give the Honey, the grandfather hands over the Water Stone automatically. If you give the Honey *first*, you must talk to the grandfather yourself **before** you see the Captain. Meeting the Captain starts the party, and the grandfather leaves the cabin for good. Safest order: Captain first, Honey second.

*Source:* file 161 (scripts 1, 3, 4; L1235, L1323, L1436, L1845), file 162 (script 4, L1551, L2797), file 156 (script 22, L4568, L6651; script 24, L6710), file 157 (script 5, L514). Cook on B1F ≈30,4 (north side, middle; the B1F stairs arrive at ≈24,3). Blue's free salt (file 156 script 22) needs flag 1422 (the cook has asked), less than $6000, no Shoal Salt (item 70) in the Bag, and flag 1440 set (the pre-party state). No script command sets 1440 before the first voyage (only 156 @6350, after the hijack; all script files scanned). The Captain's HM01 (157 script 5, @124) needs the same flag, so it must be set on boarding outside the scripts. The cook asks for salt only after the girl's request (162 script 4 checks flag 1421, set at 161 @227). The salt merchant (file 161 script 3) also sells only after 1422. Emulator (`emu_harness.py guide0107 --case ssanne`): a real boarding (zone 387 sailor, file 155 script 1, `ScrCmd_723` → zone 307), the arrival scene and the Captain's speech (coord script 5) leave 1440 clear (a fresh new game has it clear too); the Captain then shows 253#62 and Blue (with 1422, under $6000) 252#42/43. With 1440 set at that point, the Captain gives HM01 (253#55–#59) and Blue the salt (252#48). Only 156 @6350 (the hijack evacuation) sets 1440 (all script files scanned); question D-1549.

## S.S. Anne: the party battles and the missing Teddiursa

**When:** as soon as the party starts (see above). The party guests are in the 1F main hall (west side).

**How it works:**
1. The waiter with the "special Pokémon food" (hall, far west) won't serve until you've had **all six party battles**:
   - Blue (hall, north-west),
   - the "old boy" (hall, west),
   - the Raticate Gentleman (hall, west),
   - the Trainer at the north of the hall. If you already battled this Trainer in the south-east cabins before the party, that battle counts,
   - the Trainer on the west side who "had my eye on you",
   - the Psychic student of Sabrina's (hall, north).

   Blue, the old boy and the pre-party cabin battle count **whether you win or lose**. Losing to Blue or the cabin Trainer costs nothing, but losing to the old boy (or any other party Trainer) still whites you out.
2. Then the waiter offers the food, but the girl runs up: her **Teddiursa has run off**.
3. Go to the B1F kitchen. Teddiursa is eating honey from the fridge. Talk to it and it goes back to her.
4. Walk back into the hall and the Team Rocket hijack begins (story).

**Reward:** none beyond the battles (the girl and grandfather only thank you afterwards). The Raticate Gentleman also offers his Raticate for a Butterfree once beaten (an in-game trade).

**Notes:** your Pokémon didn't eat the food, so you and Green/Red fight Team Rocket in three Multi Battles in a row. Losing one whites you out to the top of the main hall, next to the stairs down to B1F, and you try again.

**Ship shop during the party:** the merchant in the hall sells Shoal Salt $1500, TM21 Frustration $4000 (the menu calls it TM63; see [Known issues](known-issues.md#vermilion-city-to-celadon-city)), Liechi Berry $800 and Ganlon Berry $800. This is the only time these berries are on sale here.

*Source:* file 156 (scripts 30–32, 34, 40; L4502, L4604, L4721, L4887; script 20 shop L4270–L4444), file 162 (script 5, script 6), file 158 (script 5). Blue (flag 1423, @4524), the old boy (1424, @4622) and the pre-party cabin battle (file 158, flag 1517) are set before the battle or without a result check. Hijack loss: L5829 whites out and warps to 1F ≈24,3.

## S.S. Anne: defuse the bomb or the ship sinks (one chance)

**Where:** B1F engine room, at the far east end of B1F. Before the hijack, a crewman there mutters about "hitting the propeller shaft from the side" and claims to be doing maintenance.

**When:** only between the hijack and your fight with Archie in the captain's cabin.

**How it works:**
1. After the hijack, go down to B1F and talk to the "maintenance" man in the engine room. He admits he planted a bomb and battles you.
2. Beat him and he removes the bomb.
3. Then go up the engine-room ladder and fight Archie. His first battle (Crawdaunt Lv. 40) can be lost: you don't black out, and Blue and Green/Red arrive either way. The final battle is still you alone against Archie (**Walrein Lv. 44, Whiscash Lv. 43, Kingdra Lv. 45**); your friends only deal with his other Pokémon in the scene afterwards. The final battle must be won (see below).

| Bomb | After Archie is beaten |
|---|---|
| **Defused** | Archie flees, the Captain retakes the ship and sails back to Vermilion. Outside, **Lt. Surge gives you a Magnet**. |
| **Not defused** | Archie escapes and the engine blows. The ship sinks; you, Blue and Green/Red survive in life jackets and wake up in the Vermilion Pokémon Center. No Magnet. |

Losing the last Archie battle also sinks the ship, even with the bomb defused: there's no white-out, the game goes straight to the sinking. So: **defuse the bomb and win the last battle.**

**Why it matters:** the sinking lasts a long time. Later NPCs in Violet City, Olivine City, Ecruteak City (including the photo-album house) and on Victory Road 3F speak as if passengers died, for example a woman whose boyfriend "can never come back". There is no retry on the ship once you've fought Archie. The only way to undo it is the Celebi trip at the Ilex Forest shrine, after your final Hall of Fame entry. You must already have received both Shaymin and Celebi at the shrine. With Celebi as your first healthy Pokémon, pick "I can't watch innocents be killed" and beat Archie there. If Celebi or Shaymin was just shown at the shrine, leave Ilex Forest and come back first.

*Source:* file 162 (script 9, L2090, L2103; only @2154 sets the "bomb defused" flag 1425), file 157 (script 4, L1710, L1834–L2134, L2197–L2852; final Archie battle is trainer 177 at @1542, a loss jumps to the sinking at L2154; the sinking sets flag 1093 at @2852); flag 1093 checked in files 52, 110, 854, 907, 916, 925. Engine-room man ≈55,7 on B1F. Undo: file 52 script 6 (Ilex Forest shrine), after the Hall of Fame (flag 2261), needs flags 2296 and 2297 set and 2298 clear and Celebi as the first non-fainted Pokémon; beating Archie (trainer 177) clears 1093 (@4858). 2298 is set whenever Celebi or Shaymin is shown at the shrine; only re-entering Ilex Forest (file 92) clears it.

## Diglett's Cave: the warm stone in the statue (after the Silver Conference)

**Where:** the broken Pokémon statue deep in the cave, in the north-east part of the lower cave, about ten steps west of the hiker who says the cave is unusually hot.

**When:** from the Silver Conference closing ceremony on (right after President Goodshow declares it closed). Before that the statue is only described. It opens at the start of the [League HQ investigation](07-league-to-cherrygrove.md#league-hq-the-trainer-affairs-departments-investigations-where-to-go-next) and stays open.

**How it works:** examine the statue → "There seems to be a hole in the top. Reach in and feel around?" → **Yes**.

**Reward:** **Magma Stone** (key item, used for [Heatran in Union Cave](08-cherrygrove-to-azalea.md#union-cave-heatran-in-the-lava-pool-tuesdays-magma-stone)). Afterwards the hiker says the cave has cooled down.

**Also here (after the final Hall of Fame):** Brock is meant to stand in the north of the cave, near the Route 2 exit, and offer a souvenir photo after your final Hall of Fame entry between 5 and 8 pm. Because of a hack bug he **can never appear**: the time check asks for the hour to be 17, 18 and 19 at the same time.

*Source:* file 5 (script 4, L532, L749; script 1, L473, L608; script 8). Statue ≈23,75; Brock ≈36,4. The statue needs var 0x40A2 ≥ 13, set right after Goodshow's closing line at the Silver Conference closing ceremony (file 107 @9518); Goodshow's briefing (file 31 @1625) sets 14. Brock: file 5 L608 hides him (flag 610) unless the hour is 17 **and** 18 **and** 19 (same pattern as the Celadon street photographer; hack finding).

## Route 11: the Swablu from the shelter

Continues [Route 5: the Pokémon shelter raid](02-pewter-to-vermilion.md#route-5-the-pokémon-shelter-raid-after-the-cascade-badge).

**Where:** the west end of Route 11, just south of the Diglett's Cave entrance. A little girl stands there with the Swablu right beside her.

**Who gets it / when:** anyone who has finished the Route 5 raid, either by winning it or by refusing to help. (Losing the raid battle only whites you out and the raid replays.) Walking up to the girl, from the west or from the north-east, starts the scene.

**How it works:**
1. A Shady Guy tries to catch the girl's (wild) Swablu so he can sell it. You're asked "Stop Swablu from being caught?".
   - **Yes:** battle **Shady Guy Wayne** (Ariados, Swellow, Girafarig, Lv. 22). Win and the girl gives you **2 Leppa Berries**. A loss whites you out and the scene replays.
   - **No:** he catches the Swablu and leaves. The girl cries, and the Swablu part of the quest is over.
2. **Returning the Swablu needs two things:** you **won** the Route 5 raid, and you **took the Pokémon you saved** (Bulbasaur for Pikachu starters, Growlithe for everyone else) from the shelter house. Until then, talking to the Swablu only gets its cry.
3. Talk to the Swablu: "Isn't this the Swablu that belongs to the girl in the house on Route 5? Take Swablu with you?" → Yes. The girl asks whether she can keep looking after it, then "Take Swablu with you?" again:

## Celadon City: the stolen Gloom (story chain, easy to get stuck)

**When:** right after you beat Erika in the Celadon Gym. Someone steals her Gloom, and the story won't move on until you find out who has it. Nothing in the dialogue tells you where the thief's auction house is, which is why so many players get stuck here.

**Before you start:** have **$50,000** for step 5. For step 4 you also need **one** of these:
- a Bulbasaur, Ivysaur, Venusaur, Charmander, Charmeleon, Charizard, Squirtle or Wartortle **in your party** (Blastoise doesn't count, see [Known issues](known-issues.md#vermilion-city-to-celadon-city)). You don't lose it;
- or **$65,535** on hand.

If you use the money route, $65,535 covers both steps: the auction boss only checks your money and doesn't take any, so you still have enough to pay the $50,000 afterwards.

**Celadon at a glance:** the north row has three doors: the Department Store (west), the **Condominiums** (middle) and the Pokémon Center (east). The Game Corner and the Prize Corner are in the middle of town, the Restaurant is south of them, and the Gym is in the southwest corner.

**Step by step:**

1. **Erika** waits outside, a few steps east of the Pokémon Center door. Talking to her is optional; she comments on each lead you find.
2. **Lead 1: the Restaurant.** Your friend (Green for male players, Red for female players) is standing right inside the entrance. Talk to them: they heard about a secret auction house for rare Pokémon.
3. **Lead 2: the Department Store, 1F.** Cynthia is a few steps up and to the right of the entrance. She saw a man in black carrying a Pokémon across the road.
   You need **both** leads before the next step. The order doesn't matter.
4. **The auction house is on the roof of the Celadon Condominiums.** No one tells you this outright (a guest in the Condominiums lobby only hints that the thief may be hiding there).
   - Enter the Condominiums (middle door of the north row) and go straight to the **elevator in the top-left corner** of the lobby. It says it's "for Game Freak only", but you can still use it. Choose **Roof**.
   - On the roof, a waiter with a Granbull stands in front of the small rooftop room on the right. With both leads he battles you: **Waiter Grant** (Granbull Lv. 36, Gyarados Lv. 37, Manectric Lv. 37). With only one lead or none, he just turns you away. Losing whites you out; come back and try again.
   - Go through the door. Talk to the boss at the back of the room and pick one of these answers:

| Answer | What happens |
|---|---|
| "Someone brought a Gloom to sell?" | He plays dumb. Doesn't work. |
| **"I'm here to buy Pokémon"** | Works if you carry **$65,535** or more (no money is taken). |
| **"I have rare Pokémon to sell"** | Works if your party holds one of the Pokémon listed above. |
| "Sorry to bother you" | Leave. |

   You can talk to him again as often as you like. A working answer gets the boss to brag that the Gloom was bought by **"the Collector"**.
5. **Lead 3: the ruined gambler.** He's on the street in the middle of town, about a dozen steps east of the Prize Corner door (below the Pokémon Center).
   - If you don't have a Coin Case, he begs for **$50,000**. Pay him and he gives you his **Coin Case** (it's the only one in the game).
   - Talk to him **again**: he tells you the Collector is **Lawrence, the owner of the Game Corner**.
   - If you talk to him **before** the auction house, he only says "Have I seen Erika's Gloom? Isn't it in the Gym?". That doesn't mean he's the wrong person; come back after step 4.
6. **Go back to Erika** (east of the Pokémon Center) and tell her. The search ends, and the story moves on to the Game Corner and the Team Rocket hideout below it.

**If you're stuck, check:**
- The waiter on the roof only turns you away: you're missing one of the two leads (step 2 or 3).
- The auction boss throws you out: you don't have one of the listed Pokémon in your **party** (the PC doesn't count) and you're carrying less than $65,535.
- The gambler won't talk about the Collector: you haven't got a working answer from the auction boss yet, or you don't have the Coin Case.
- Not enough money: win trainer battles or sell items. Without the money (or one of the listed Pokémon) the story can't continue.

*Source:* script files 783 (scripts 24, 25; L2992, L3059, L3081, L3103, L3125, L4521, L4633, L6061), 787 (script 5), 803 (script 8), 794 (script 7, lobby hint), 797 (script 3, waiter), 798 (script 1, auction boss), 799 (left Condominiums elevator); gym win clears flag 1179 (file 784 L1392); the waiter and auction staff appear after the Route 7 scene (file 853 L1840 clears flag 1797). Positions read from the map events (Celadon events 52, 327, 333, 336, 337, 340).

## Celadon Rocket warehouse: the secret passage and the Persian statue password

**Where:** the Team Rocket base under the Celadon Game Corner. You go in with Misty.

**How it works:**
1. **Launcher program:** a computer on the first floor, about ten steps west of the stairs you come down from the Game Corner, runs the secret passage program; a cabinet slides away to reveal the stairs down (the Game Corner scene must have happened first).
2. **The password:** a Persian statue at the far west side of a lower floor blocks the lab. The control computer, at the far east side next to where James stands, wants a five-digit password, each digit 1–6. Clues on the floor:
   - a document on a computer next to a device that keeps going "Morse... Morse...": "Stored separately on five devices. Arrange them from bottom to top and from right to left";
   - a device flashing six colours in order: red, orange, yellow, green, blue, purple (= 1–6);
   - five devices blinking Morse for a colour: RED=1, ORA=2, YEL=3, GRE=4, BLU=5, PUR=6. The cryptographer in the Celadon Pokémon Center gives the Morse letters you need.
   - The devices change with the **day of the week**, and the last one with the **hour**.

   Just enter the code for the in-game weekday (on the website, the calculator below the table works it out from the day and hour):

| Weekday | Password |
|---|---|
| Sunday | 1 6 5 4 **x** |
| Monday, Tuesday | 2 4 3 1 **x** |
| Wednesday | 4 3 6 5 **x** |
| Thursday | 5 4 1 2 **x** |
| Friday | 2 5 1 3 **x** |
| Saturday | 3 4 6 5 **x** |

   **x** depends on the hour: 00–03 → 1, 04–07 → 2, 08–11 → 3, 12–15 → 4, 16–19 → 5, 20–23 → 6.
   A wrong digit isn't flagged until the end ("Incorrect password"); retry as often as you like.
