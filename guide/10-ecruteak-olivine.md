## Route 35: the Shuckle juice-maker wants a Jigglypuff (→ 2 Lum Berries)

**Where:** Route 35, the man with two Shuckle, a few steps north-east of the gatehouse you come out of from Goldenrod City.

**Who gets it / when:** any time. One time only.

**How it works:**
1. He wants a Jigglypuff to sing his Shuckle to sleep so they can brew Berry Juice.
2. Talk to him with a **Jigglypuff** in your party. "Give him the Jigglypuff in your party?" → Yes, then pick it from the party screen.
3. It must be a Jigglypuff, not an Egg, and not your only Pokémon. Any other choice just gets a complaint, and you can try again.

**Reward:** **2 Lum Berries**. The Jigglypuff is gone for good; it then stands next to him.

*Source:* script file 240 (Route 35; script 14, L2823–L4176; flag 418). Man at ≈366,310.

## Route 35: the Pidgey with a letter (starts a chain in Ecruteak City)

**Where:** Route 35, the hungry Pidgey on the east side of the route, in the northern half (about a dozen steps south-east of the north gatehouse, and 8 steps north of the Ace Trainer with a Glaceon).

**Who gets it / when:** any time.

**How it works:**
1. "This Pidgey seems very hungry. Feed it an Oran Berry?" → Yes. You need an **Oran Berry** in your Bag (it isn't taken; the game only checks that you have one). Without one: "You don't have enough Oran Berries!"
2. Pidgey recovers. "The Pidgey seems to be carrying a letter. Read it?" → Yes. The writer's health keeps her indoors, so she can only make friends by Pidgey post. She asks you to **come to Ecruteak City** and tell her stories from your journey.
3. Pidgey flies off. If you answer No at step 2, talk to it again to read the letter.
4. The writer lives in Ecruteak City, in the house just east of the Pokémon Center. Until you read the letter she only says that her Pidgey hasn't come back. For what happens there, see [the Ecruteak City Pidgey letter entry](#ecruteak-city-the-wheelchair-bound-writers-pidgey-letter--wise-glasses-continues-route-35s-hungry-pidgey) below.

**Reward:** none here.

*Source:* script file 240 (script 18, L1529–L4105; HasItem 155 at L1559, no TakeItem in the file; flags 1847, 1846; L4099 clears flag 1061), 925 (Ecruteak, script 15 L637–L1197 checks flag 1061). Letter text 380#44 (health keeps her indoors; no child). Pidgey at ≈375,278; writer's house door ≈407,183 (not the Pichu house at ≈386,183).

## Route 35: Granbull kid "Dog Fangs" (→ Dragon Scale)

**Where:** Route 35, the Battle Girl with a Granbull, on the east side of the route about halfway along.

**Who gets it / when:** any time. One time only.

**How it works:** "Will you play with me for a while?" → Yes, then "Can we have a Pokémon battle?" → Yes. Battle Girl Zia: Granbull, Crawdaunt, Mismagius, Zangoose, Lv. 62–65. Losing whites you out, and you can try again.

**Reward:** **Dragon Scale**.

*Source:* script file 240 (script 17, L1492–L3372; flag 1778; trainer 783). Battle Girl at ≈371,297.

## National Park → Route 35: "the girl on a date with an old man" (→ 3 Heart Mail)

**Where:** starts with the heartbroken boy in the south-east corner of Route 35, about 20 steps east of the gatehouse from Goldenrod City. The couple are at the north end of the National Park, a few steps east of the sign near the north exit.

**Who gets it / when:** any time (the couple are hidden during the Bug-Catching Contest).

**How it works:**
1. The boy on Route 35 heard that the girl he likes is on a date with an old man in the National Park. Until you've done step 2 he only laments.
2. In the National Park, talk to the rich man with the young woman: "Take a look at Uncle's little girlfriend here. Young and pretty, isn't she?" Answer **No**. He takes offence and battles you: Gentleman Mr. Benz, Tentacruel, Aggron, Cacturne, Purugly (Lv. 62–65). Losing whites you out. ("Yes" just gets a boast, and you can ask again.)
3. Go back to the boy and pick **"She's with an old man"** ("Never mind" does nothing; ask again).

**Reward:** **3 Heart Mail** (the present he meant for her).

**Notes:** the young woman calls him "my uncle", who helped her and her mother after her father died. After the battle she recognises you as the Champion and flirts (the text changes with your gender). Once you've told the boy, he only sobs.

*Source:* script files 25 (National Park, scripts 39–40, L3600–L3659; flag 1917; trainer 794), 240 (script 16, L1354–L3309; menu 46/47; flag 1718). Boy at ≈380,319; couple at ≈51,16.

## Route 36 gatehouse → National Park: the mother and the "puppy love" (two endings)

**Where:** the mother in the National Park gatehouse on Route 36 (just inside the Route 36 door), and two school kids with a Weepinbell and a Bellossom in the west of the National Park.

**Who gets it / when:** any time you meet her; she doesn't check for a Badge. In practice you can only get here after the Plain Badge, when Routes 35 and 36 open. The quest shares its progress counter with the Goldenrod Gym chain; see Notes.

**How it works:**
1. The mother saw her son go into the National Park with a girl and asks you to catch his "puppy love" red-handed. Answer **Yes**. (Her son is the grade-schooler with glasses.)
2. Walk up to the kids in the park (their Weepinbell is courting the girl's Bellossom). "Catch them in the act?" → Yes. They explain their Pokémon are the couple, not them. You choose:

| Choice | What happens | Reward |
|---|---|---|
| **"You'll be great Trainers"** | You promise to handle the grown-ups. Go back to the mother and explain it was a school project. Then talk to the boy again. | **Sport Ball** (from the boy) |
| **"Excuses mean it's true"** | The kids battle you together: School Kids Dean and Hannah, Weepinbell and Bellossom Lv. 28 (Double Battle). Win and you march the boy back to his mother, who scolds him. | **Dawn Stone** (from the mother) |

**Notes:**
- The harsh ending is final: the boy and his Weepinbell leave the park and stand next to his mother in the gatehouse, and the girl sits alone with a listless Bellossom.
- Losing the kids' battle whites you out; you can try again.
- The mother's and kids' progress is kept in the same counter as the Goldenrod Gym Multi Battle chain (Radio Tower → Gold → Norman). If you accepted her request while that chain was still running, the chain's progress would be overwritten (Whitney restarts it from "want the Plain Badge?"). In normal play this can't happen, because this area only opens after the Plain Badge.

*Source:* script files 245 (Route 36 gatehouse, script 4, L655–L1076; var 0x408d 5→7→8 / 6; values 0–4 all get the request, no Badge check; script 5 L788 moves the boy and Weepinbell to (6,6)/(5,6) beside the mother when var = 6), 25 (National Park, script 1 L184–L2533, L3716–L4375, L5199–L5297; var 0x408d → 9; flag 548; trainers 784/785), 883 (Goldenrod Gym: var 0x408d 1–4). Mother at ≈7,6 in the gatehouse; kids at ≈24,56 in the park.

## Route 36: the odd tree (after the Plain Badge)

**Where:** Route 36, the odd tree at the junction west of the Violet City gatehouse.

**Who gets it / when:** story. Nothing moves the tree before the Plain Badge. After it, **Crystal and Gold** (with Bayleef and Quilava) wait just south of the tree.

**How it works:** talk to **Crystal**. She got a Squirt Bottle from Ms. Saki at the Goldenrod Flower Shop and waters the tree. It turns out to be a **Sudowoodo**, which runs off. The way north to Ecruteak City (and east to Violet City) is open.

**Reward:** the road.

**Notes:**
- You don't need the Squirt Bottle yourself; Crystal does it. Using Cut on the tree does nothing.
- An unmasked Sudowoodo stands further west on Route 36, about a dozen steps east of Lily and her uncle by the National Park gate. It's already there from the Plain Badge, before the tree scene (a quirk; see [Known issues](known-issues.md#prof-birch-and-the-unmasked-sudowoodo)). It won't budge until you do the Rock Incense quest below.
- To be safe, do this scene **before your first visit inside the Pokéathlon Dome** (see [Known issues](known-issues.md#radio-tower-ecruteak-city-and-olivine-city)).

*Source:* script file 243 (Route 36, script 14, L634–L1217; flags 551, 463), 883 (Goldenrod Gym L617–L621 clears flags 111 and 551). Tree at ≈415,246; the second Sudowoodo at ≈392,235 (object 20) is hidden only by flag 111, which the Plain Badge clears (file 883 L617) and only the Rock Incense quest sets (file 243 L3814).

## Route 36: Lily's uncle and the Sudowoodo (Rock Incense → $10,000 + Power Lens)

**Where:** Route 36, the man and the little girl Lily, a few steps north-east of the National Park gatehouse door. The Sudowoodo stands about a dozen steps east of them.

**Who gets it / when:** after the odd-tree scene, with a **Rock Incense** in your Bag (from [the girl at the Ruins of Alph](08-cherrygrove-to-azalea.md#ruins-of-alph-the-girl-who-saw-her-own-face--rock-incense), or an item ball in Diglett's Cave).

**How it works:**
1. The uncle boasts he's an expert catcher; Lily wants him to catch the Sudowoodo from the odd tree.
2. Talk to him while carrying the Rock Incense. The Sudowoodo is drawn to its scent and walks over, and Lily thinks her uncle called it.
3. He admits he can't catch Pokémon and begs you to sell him the Rock Incense so Sudowoodo will stay. **You can't really refuse:** "No" just repeats the question.

**Reward:** **$10,000** and a **Power Lens**. The Sudowoodo stays with Lily (it's not catchable).

*Source:* script file 243 (script 21, L2127–L3824; item 315; flags 137, 111). Uncle at ≈379,235; Sudowoodo at ≈392,235.

## Route 36 gatehouse → Blackthorn City: the runaway Dragonair Trainer (→ Play Rough tutor)

**Where:** the Violet City–Route 36 gatehouse, the Ace Trainer near the west door. His parents live in Blackthorn City. The Move Deleter's door is the westmost door in the row with the Poké Mart and Pokémon Center; the parents' door is about ten steps north of it, on the west side of town (not the Move Deleter's room).

**Who gets it / when:** any time; you need to reach Blackthorn City to finish it.

**How it works:**
1. Talk to him: he's the Dragonair user you beat in the underground battles in Kanto. He left Blackthorn almost three years ago, has won hardly any Badges and is too ashamed to go home.
2. In Blackthorn City, go into his parents' house and talk to the **father** (a few steps straight ahead from the door). He tells you their son left to train with his Dragonair and never came back.
3. Return to the gatehouse and talk to him. "Convince him to go home?" → **Yes**. He goes home.
4. Back in Blackthorn, his father thanks you.

**Reward:** his father offers to teach **Play Rough** (free, any time you ask). The move he actually teaches is **[Flail](/moves/flail/)** (a hack bug; see [Known issues](known-issues.md#lake-of-rage-blackthorn-city-and-beyond)).

**Notes:** if you haven't talked to his father yet, he only tells his story.

*Source:* script files 862 (gatehouse, script 3, L181–L674; flags 391, 390, 513), 944 (Blackthorn Move Tutor house, script 8, L463–L481 and L787–L1001). Ace Trainer at ≈3,6 in the gatehouse. The parent at ≈37,5 is the father (626#54 我和孩子他妈, "his mother and I"). His room is reached from Blackthorn door ≈660,165; the Move Deleter's room is door ≈658,176. The tutor teaches move 175 (Flail) instead of Play Rough: decision D-1305.

## National Park: the Bug-Catching Contest

**Where:** the National Park; register with the officer in either National Park gatehouse (Route 35 or Route 36 side).

**Who gets it / when:** **Tuesdays, Thursdays and Saturdays** only, once per day.

**How it works:** the usual HeartGold contest. You enter with one Pokémon (the lead) and 20 Sport Balls. Your other Pokémon are held for you; with a full party the PC must have space. Catch one Bug Pokémon and keep the best one. Only Sport Balls work in the park during the contest. Time runs out or you can quit early at the gate.

**Reward:** a prize for first, second and third place and a consolation prize for everyone else, picked by the game itself rather than by a person you talk to. Which items this hack hands out isn't confirmed yet. If your Bag is full, the officer keeps your prize for your next visit.

**Notes:** during the contest most of the park's usual visitors (the couples, Will and Karen, the Charm tutor) are hidden.

*Source:* script files 242, 245 (gatehouses; weekday checks at file 242 L205–L1492), 151 (common contest script: registration L253–L612, judging L832–L1268; flag 2727 = entered today).

## Burned Tower → Bell Tower gate: bring Morty to the Marauder (story; the gate quiz)

**Where:** Burned Tower (north-west Ecruteak), the Bell Tower gatehouse (at the north end of town, east of the Burned Tower), Bellchime Trail behind it.

**Who gets it / when:** the Ecruteak story chapter, after Silver's scene on Route 37.

**How it works:**
1. In the Burned Tower basement a man in a red cape (the **Marauder**) is about to smash the Sealing Mirror. Pick the option to stop him. He says he'll only listen to Morty. "I'll give you today…" is only talk: there is no real time limit.
2. Go to the Bell Tower gatehouse. The guard at the back says the tower is closed. Choose **"I'm the Champion. I need Morty."**, then **Yes** to answer his questions. All six must be right in one go; one wrong answer ends it ("Wrong answer…") and you start again from question 1. The old man on the left tells the legend if you want hints.

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| What did Ho-Oh later do for the three Pokémon that burned to death? | **Revived them** |
| Which three Pokémon are the legendary beasts? | **Raikou/Entei/Suicune** |
| What did Ho-Oh do to them to keep them from clashing with people? | **Sealed them in a mirror** |
| What did Ho-Oh choose to do itself? | **Left Ecruteak City** |
| When will Ho-Oh and the beasts return? | **When people need them** |
| What kind of Pokémon is Ho-Oh? | **Kind and forgiving** |

3. Walk up Bellchime Trail to Morty and Eusine (cutscene).
4. Back in the Burned Tower: Gold and Silver at the entrance, then in the basement the Marauder breaks the seal and Team Rocket attacks. You fight **Marauder and Maxie together in a Double Battle** on your own. Losing whites you out; the scene replays.

**Reward:** the story moves on. Afterwards Morty's Gym opens (until then a man a few steps inside the entrance says Morty is away at the Bell Tower, and he stands in the only way in), and Gold's training (below), the Aura Sphere tutor in the gatehouse and the Poltergeist disciple in the Gym appear.

*Source:* script file 23 (script 5 L3965–L5402 sets 0x4098 = 4; script 4 sets 6; script 1 L1482 trainers 800 + 822, L3517 sets 7 and flag 462), file 922 (script 1, L370–L947, flag 457), file 22 (script 7, sets 0x4098 = 5), file 918 obj 6 (flag 462). Gatehouse door ≈392,153. The Gym man (≈16,49, hidden by flag 462) stands on the only tile leading onto the invisible floor.

## Ecruteak Gym: Morty's Ghost challenge (the Fog Badge)

**Where:** Ecruteak Gym (south-west of town). Morty is at the back, past the invisible-floor maze.

**Who gets it / when:** after the Burned Tower chapter above. See Notes for when Morty refuses.

**How it works:** Morty only battles a **full party of six** from his accepted list (meant to be Psychic- and Ghost-types). The match is a **Double Battle** against Morty's six, sent out two at a time (see [Known issues](known-issues.md#mortys-lv-1-pokémon) for its Lv. 1 members).
- Fewer than six Pokémon: "You're carrying fewer than six Pokémon…"
- Any Pokémon he doesn't accept: "Some of the Pokémon you're carrying…". The game checks a fixed list of species, not types (Eggs are skipped by the species check). Psychic and Ghost lines from Gens 1–4 are accepted, plus **Golduck** and **Noctowl** (Psychic in this hack). **Exeggutor and Rotom are refused.** Stunfisk, Yungoos and Gumshoos also slip through.
- Full list from Gens 1–4: Abra line, Slowpoke line (incl. Slowking), Gastly line, Drowzee, Hypno, Exeggcute, Starmie, Mr. Mime, Mime Jr., Jynx, Smoochum, Mewtwo, Mew, Natu, Xatu, Espeon, Misdreavus, Mismagius, Unown, Wobbuffet, Wynaut, Girafarig, Lugia, Celebi, Ralts line, Gallade, Shedinja, Sableye, Meditite, Medicham, Spoink, Grumpig, Lunatone, Solrock, Baltoy, Claydol, Shuppet, Banette, Duskull line, Chimecho, Chingling, Beldum line, Latias, Latios, Jirachi, Deoxys, Drifloon, Drifblim, Bronzor, Bronzong, Spiritomb, Froslass, Uxie, Mesprit, Azelf, Giratina, Cresselia, Golduck, Noctowl. Exeggutor and Rotom are refused, although they are Psychic/Ghost.
- Losing whites you out; try again.

**Reward:** **Fog Badge**, **TM30 Shadow Ball**, Morty's number. Afterwards Morty offers repeatable practice battles (Single or Double, no restrictions), and his disciple by the door teaches **Poltergeist** (free, Ghost-types).

## Olivine Gym: Jasmine's rule (three Pokémon with base Defense 130+)

**Where:** Olivine Gym. Jasmine is at the back.

**Who gets it / when:** after Gold's battle at the south end of Route 39 (he's just won the Mineral Badge). This is the Mineral Badge.

- **Every** Pokémon must be on her list. The game checks a fixed list of species meant to match base Defense 130+, for example Steelix, Skarmory, Forretress, Shuckle, Aggron, Metagross, Bastiodon and Probopass. **Aegislash is refused**, though Doublade is allowed, and a few weak species slip through (Stunfisk, Yungoos and Gumshoos; see [Known issues](known-issues.md#radio-tower-ecruteak-city-and-olivine-city)).
