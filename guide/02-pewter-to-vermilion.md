## Pewter City: finding Brock (the Gym Leader's errands)

**Where:** Pewter Gym, then the **house in the northeast of town** (about 21 steps east and 5 steps north of the Gym door), then the Museum of Science, then Anemone's flower garden on the hill.

**Who gets it / when:** everyone. This is required for the Boulder Badge. Players get stuck because Brock keeps leaving.

**How it works:**
1. *(Optional)* In the northeast house, the woman says her Nidoran♂ keeps throwing up and asks you to fetch Brock.
2. Talk to Brock in the Gym. He hurries off to that northeast house. If you did step 1, you pass on the message. If not, he says he just heard about it himself.
3. Northeast house: talk to Brock while he treats Nidoran♂. He then leaves "to go back to the Gym".
4. The Gym guide now says Brock took his little brother and sister to the **museum**.
5. Museum: talk to Brock. He asks you to keep his siblings busy. Answer **both** quizzes:

   **Brother (fossil quiz):** a wrong answer doesn't stop the quiz. You still hear every question and are told at the end ("that doesn't sound right"). Retry at once.
   | # | Question | Answer (position) |
   |---|---|---|
   | 1 | Fewest type weaknesses? | **Anorith** (2nd) |
   | 2 | How many Fossil Pokémon are not Rock types? | **0** (1st) |
   | 3 | Highest base Defense? | **Bastiodon** (4th) |
   | 4 | Which is not a Fossil Pokémon? | **Relicanth** (3rd) |

   **Sister ("my big brother's acting weird"):**
   | # | Question | Answer (position) |
   |---|---|---|
   | 1 | What is he secretly writing? | **Cheesy love lines** (3rd) |
   | 2 | Why does he keep spacing out? | **He's in love** (3rd) |
   | 3 | What do we mean in his life? | **A loving family** (2nd) |
   | 4 | Why is he acting so strange? | **Because of love** (4th) |

   Like her brother, she asks every question even after a wrong answer and only tells you at the end ("My woman's intuition tells me that's not right"). Retry at once. Declining her ("How can you refuse...") just ends the talk.
6. Talk to Brock again. He thanks you and leaves. The siblings go back to the Gym and mention a "goddess".
7. Walk up to **Anemone's garden**, about 16 steps east of the Pokémon Center door. The cutscene starts when you walk onto the path 4 steps south of Anemone: Brock is hitting on Anemone, you step in, and he heads back to the Gym.
8. Gym: Brock finally battles you. Losing whites you out; you can try again.

**Reward:** the Boulder Badge (story). The treated Nidoran♂ has no reward.

*Source:* files 750 (Gym, L485/L850), 754 (script 1, script 3), 753 (scripts 16, 17, 20; L3143, L3543, L4622 sets 1315 and clears 1316), 748 (script 27, L4562 clears 1322). Step 1 sets flag 1321. The Nidoran♂ room is the west half (local 4,8) of the shared "Pewter Northeast house" map, entered from the city door at 1068,87; the garden cutscene is the step trigger at 1064–1065,111, var 0x4082 = 1 (Pewter events: Gym door 1047,92, Anemone 1064,107, Pokémon Center door 1048,106).

## Pewter City: the Apricorn Ball maker

Continues [Route 2 East: Nidoran♀ and the Apricorn Ball maker](01-pallet-to-pewter.md#route-2-east-nidoran-and-the-apricorn-ball-maker-non-pikachu-starters).

**Where:** the small house in the southwest of town, about 11 steps west and 5 steps south of the Pokémon Center door.

**Who gets it / when:** Bulbasaur and Charmander starters who saved Nidoran♀ on Route 2. Pikachu starters find him here from the start, with no Nidoran♀ story ("I learned to make Apricorn Balls from Mr. Kurt").

**How it works:** talk to him with Apricorns in your Bag. He takes one, and the ball is ready **about a day later**. Then talk to him again to collect it. It's free.

**Notes:**
- He and Kurt in Azalea share the same "ball in progress" slot, so only one of them can be making a ball at a time. His balls also count toward Kurt's ball counter. Reaching 10 balls has no effect that we could find.
- **The service ends for good** once you've been through the Slowpoke Well event in Johto. After that he says he's putting ball-making aside to spend time with Nidoran♀.
- If you let the breeder take Nidoran♀, he just mopes and never makes balls.

*Source:* file 754 (script 4, L697, L829, L840); flags 415 (file 60, Slowpoke Well), 2722 and 0x413b (file 871). Saved Nidoran♀ = flag 1349 clear. Ball in progress = flag 2722, 10-ball counter = var 0x413b, which sets flag 295 at 10 (no script checks 295). Service ends when flag 415 is set. The room is the east half (local 36,8) of the shared "Pewter Northeast house" map, entered from the city door at 1037,111, not from the northeast door (warp anchors checked).

## Pewter Pokémon Center: Elite Four Bruno (one attempt, before Mt. Moon)

**Where:** Bruno and his Hitmonchan stand about 12 steps straight north of the Pokémon Center entrance.

**How it works:** answer Yes to "Challenge Bruno?". His team is Lv. 87–88: Hitmontop, Machamp, Steelix, Hitmonlee, Hitmonchan and Infernape.

**Notes:** you get **one try**. The game marks him as done *before* the battle, and losing doesn't white you out. Win or lose, afterwards he only praises your spirit. He leaves once the Mt. Moon roadblock story ends. There's no item reward.

*Source:* file 751 (script 8, L612). "Done" flag 1328 is set before the battle; he is hidden by flag 1311. Position 7,7 (door 8,19).

## Route 3: the old painter's Ralts (Fresh Water errand; before the Cascade Badge)

**Where:** an old man with a Ralts, southwest of the Route 3 Pokémon Center: about 37 steps west and 18 steps south of its door, a few steps south of an item ball.

**How it works:**
1. He's ill and moved here for the Mt. Moon spring water. Talk to him with **Fresh Water** in your Bag. He drinks it (1 Fresh Water used) and gives you a **Big Root**.
2. Talk again. He asks you to take his late wife's Ralts on your journey. Answer Yes with a free party slot.

**Reward:** Big Root, then **Ralts, Lv. 15**. Answering No or having a full party lets you ask again.

**Getting Fresh Water:** the Mt. Moon Square shop sells it for $600, but only after the Mt. Moon roadblock story. The shop stops selling it while Asher's gang holds the spring (see the Mt. Moon Square entry). So in practice you buy it between the roadblock and your Cascade Badge.

**Missable:** when you win the Cascade Badge (Misty hands you TM03 right after), the painter passes away. If you haven't taken Ralts by then, it stays behind with a letter. Read it and say Yes to "Take Ralts with you?" to fight a **wild Lv. 15 Ralts**. Catch it: knocking it out also removes it for good. Losing whites you out and you can retry. The Big Root is gone once he has died.

*Source:* file 175 (script 9, L2396, L2409, L4238; letter L2278); file 758 L3883 sets 1390 (the painter's death); file 6 (shop, needs flag 1994). Fresh Water is not a Route 3 field item (work/docs/items.md lists Route 3 only as where it's needed; the item ball near the painter is TM27, item 354). Position 1130,124; Pokémon Center door 1167,106; item ball 1132,120.

## Route 3: the Scyther chase (Charmander starters) / Bug Catcher Kenji (others)

**Where:** a Bug Catcher about 50 steps west of the Route 3 Pokémon Center door, a few steps east of a man who talks about Dusk Balls. The ledge is about 10 steps west and 7 steps south of him.

**Charmander starters:** he says he chased a Scyther here from Viridian Forest but can't beat it. The Scyther then appears on the ledge. Check it: "The Scyther is looking down haughtily. Challenge it?" → Yes starts a wild battle against **Scyther, Lv. 15**. If you lose, you white out and can retry. Winning or catching removes it.

**Other starters:** he already owns Scyther. Answer Yes to battle him (Scyther Lv. 11, Pinsir Lv. 10). This is a one-time battle with no item.

*Source:* file 175 (scripts 30, 31; L3512, L3608). Talking to him clears flag 2268 to show the ledge Scyther. Bug Catcher ≈1117,104, ledge ≈1107,111, Pokémon Center door 1167,106.

## Route 3: Misty's Gyarados, "Comfort Misty?" (romance choice)

**Where:** it starts on your way east along Route 3, when you walk past the Trainer standing about 18 steps west and 13 steps south of the Route 3 Pokémon Center door (anywhere in the column running from him to 4 steps south of him).

**How it works:** Misty's runaway Gyarados charges you. You battle it (a Lv. 65 Gyarados). The story goes on the same way whether you win or lose, and losing doesn't white you out. Then Misty apologises and asks "Comfort Misty?".

**Warning:** answering No closes Misty's romance route (see below).

- **Yes:** you encourage her and have a battle with her (Starmie Lv. 12, Goldeen Lv. 11, Horsea Lv. 10, Psyduck Lv. 5). Then she goes home.
- **No:** she goes home without a battle, **and this closes Misty's romance route** (her confession in the Cerulean Gym, in the final chapter, checks it). Only the Island Forest wish on Six Island, made before the Dream World, reopens it ([the romance route's central entry](09-ilex-goldenrod.md#romance-route-how-its-unlocked-how-your-partner-is-chosen-and-what-locks-a-partner-out-central-entry)).

**Notes:** either answer puts Misty in Mt. Moon for the roadblock scene.

*Source:* file 175 (script 17, L3346, L4102); file 758 L7072 (confession check); file 55 L3039 (Six Island wish clears 2142). "No" sets flag 2142; both answers clear flag 1372 (Misty in Mt. Moon). Step trigger at x 1149, z 119–123 (var 0x4086 = 0); Trainer object at 1149,119.

## Mt. Moon: what opens the fake guards' roadblock

**Where:** the "construction" guards blocking the way at the north end of Mt. Moon's first cave, about 37 steps north and 11 steps west of the Route 3 entrance.

**Why players get stuck:** the guard won't start the scene until **all four** companions are waiting in the cave and you have the **Boulder Badge**. Until then he only repeats "The road ahead is under construction!".

| Companion | What puts them there |
|---|---|
| Blue | your rival battle in Pewter City, on the path between the Pokémon Center and the Gym (story; it starts on your way to the Gym) |
| Misty | the Route 3 Gyarados scene (previous entry; either answer) |
| **Steven** | **beat Steven at the Pewter Museum** (Beldum Lv. 12, Baltoy Lv. 11, Aron Lv. 12). Answer Yes when he offers, then talk to him again inside Mt. Moon: take the passage on the east side of the first cave down to the inner cave, where he stands about 7 steps east of where you arrive, next to an archaeologist. No just ends the talk ("That's a real shame") and you can ask again. |
| Cynthia | talk to her at the first fork in Mt. Moon, about 11 steps north of the Route 3 entrance, and answer her "left or right?" question. Any answer works. |

Once all four are there, they wait just south of the guards. Then talk to the guard. Green (Red for female players) exposes them as Team Rocket, and you fight four Multi Battles, one beside each companion. That leads on to the Mt. Moon Square Mew scene.

**Notes:** Nurse Joy inside the cave heals you before and after. Steven is also in the Cerulean Bike Shop until you beat Misty: talking to him starts a battle straight away (Metang Lv. 20, Aron Lv. 15, Baltoy Lv. 16, Anorith Lv. 17). It's a one-time battle with no item, and losing whites you out.

## Mt. Moon: the fossil seller

**Where:** a Super Nerd digging by a fossil in Mt. Moon's inner cave, about 15 steps east and 4 steps north of the exit to Route 4 (and about 17 steps south and 13 steps west of Steven's spot from the previous entry).

**How it works:** checking the fossil makes him battle you (Super Nerd Zack: Magnemite Lv. 16, Voltorb Lv. 17, Nosepass Lv. 18). Losing whites you out; check the fossil again to retry. After you win, talk to him: he sells **Helix Fossil** or **Dome Fossil** for **$5,000 each**, as often as you like.

*Source:* file 133 (scripts 7, 8; L2149, L2216). Fossil ≈28,70, Route 4 exit 13,74.

## Mt. Moon Square: Asher's gang and the spring (after the Cascade Badge)

**Where:** Mt. Moon Square. Asher and his two girlfriends block the spring, about 10 steps west and 3 steps south of the shop door.

**Who gets it / when:** once you've won the Cascade Badge from Misty. The Square's visitors reappear and Asher's gang moves in. The shop stops selling Fresh Water for now.

**How it works:**
1. Talk to any visitor who complains about the gang: the couple just southwest of Asher, the old woman or the old man (a couple of steps left of the shop door and a few steps down), or the clerk inside the shop. Before you do, Asher only asks you politely to wait.
2. Talk to Asher and battle him: Rich Boy Asher, Haunter/Skiploom/Spinda/Lopunny, all Lv. 25. Losing whites you out. Win and the gang leaves.
3. Collect the thank-you gifts.

**Reward:**
- **Destiny Knot:** from whichever of the couple southwest of Asher you talk to first. The other one only sings.
- **Everstone:** from the old woman (the upper one of the old pair near the shop door).
- The old man only reminisces.
- The shop sells Fresh Water again ($600).

**Notes:** the couple about 11 steps east and 5 steps south of the shop door (also there only after the Cascade Badge) gives a **Soothe Bell** after their little skit, whether or not you beat Asher. The Clefairy Moonblast tutor (costs 1 Moon Stone) is also hidden until the Cascade Badge, and only dances at night.

*Source:* file 9 (scripts 7, 10–14; L1647, L1863, L1905, L1958), file 6 (shop), file 758 L3895/L3899 (TM03 scene clears flags 1387 and 1994). Talking to a complaining visitor sets flag 1232; beating Asher sets flag 1994. Positions: Asher and girlfriends ≈17–19,46–47; couple ≈16,50–51; old woman ≈26,46, old man ≈26,47; Soothe Bell couple ≈39–40,48; shop door 28,43.

## Mt. Moon: Groudon (post-game, Red Orb)

**Where:** the altar in the deepest part of Mt. Moon's inner cave, where Maxie summoned Groudon. You reach that part by the passage in the far northeast of the inner cave. From where you arrive, the altar is about 28 steps east and 15 steps north.

**Who gets it / when:** after your final Hall of Fame entry, with the **Red Orb** in your Bag. After that entry you wake up at home in Pallet Town, and Lance brings you the legendary items Team Rocket had seized, the Red Orb among them ([Pallet Town: Lance's visit home](01-pallet-to-pewter.md#pallet-town-lances-visit-home-after-the-final-hall-of-fame)). This is after the Team Rocket Groudon story.

**How it works:** check the altar: "Try summoning Groudon with the Red Orb?" → Yes starts a wild battle against **Groudon, Lv. 95**.

**Notes:** losing whites you out, and you can try again. **Knocking it out counts like catching it: Groudon is gone for good.**

*Source:* file 133 (script 13, L2304, L2351; needs flag 2261 = final Hall of Fame and item 534 Red Orb); flag 2287 set on catch or knockout; file 842 L1900 (Lance gives the Red Orb). Altar ≈37,115; arrival warp 9,130 (from 57,16). The ball-sprite object at 36,115 is a cutscene prop (hidden by scratch flag 1366), not a pick-up.

## Route 5: the Pokémon shelter raid (after the Cascade Badge)

**Where:** outside the Pokémon shelter house on Route 5. The scene starts when you walk south down the middle of the path toward the house, about 11 steps north of its door. The side lanes to the west and east of the house skip it, so walk straight down the middle.

**Who gets it / when:** after the Cascade Badge. **Deadline:** if the raid hasn't happened by the time Sabrina laughs in Pokémon Tower ([Vermilion City to Celadon City](03-vermilion-to-celadon.md#pokémon-tower-make-sabrina-laugh-the-marsh-badge-trial)), it's lost: the Pokémon disappear and there's no reward.

**How it works:** a Team Rocket grunt tries to steal the shelter girl's Pokémon. Her Swablu escapes. The Pokémon at risk is **Bulbasaur for Pikachu starters** and **Growlithe for everyone else**. The game asks "This looks bad. Step in and help?".

**Warning:** No loses the Pokémon for good, and with it the Cleanse Tag, the Magical Leaf tutor and the Swablu reward on Route 11.

- **Yes:** battle the grunt (Mightyena Lv. 20 with a Choice Band, Sudowoodo Lv. 20, Granbull Lv. 23). Win to save the Pokémon. Losing whites you out and the raid plays again.
- **No:** the grunt takes it away. It doesn't come back.

**Reward (if you saved it):** inside the house:
- The saved Pokémon wants to travel with you. Talk to it and answer Yes to "Take Bulbasaur/Growlithe along on your journey?" to get **Bulbasaur or Growlithe, Lv. 15** (needs a free party slot).
- The grandfather gives you a **Cleanse Tag**. After that he also teaches **Magical Leaf** for free, as often as you like.

**Follow-up:** the escaped Swablu turns up on **Route 11** ([Vermilion City to Celadon City](03-vermilion-to-celadon.md#route-11-the-swablu-from-the-shelter)). To return it you must have won the raid **and** taken the saved Pokémon along. Bring the Swablu home for **3 PP Max**, or let the Route 11 girl keep it for **1 PP Max** from the shelter girl plus **2 Chesto Berries** from the Route 11 girl.

*Source:* file 179 (script 3, L1283, L2144; trainer 966; which Pokémon is decided by flag 1037/1038, set in file 9 L5646), file 183 (scripts 1–4; L669, L904 Cleanse Tag needs raid-won flag 305, L1037 Magical Leaf); file 758 L8040 clears flag 1043 and resets var 0x409e (Cascade Badge); file 17 @12165 closes an unfinished raid (Sabrina scene). Route 11 is file 197 (flags 1445/1451; it checks 1046 and 1047, which file 9 L5538/L5542 sets and the raid win clears for the saved Pokémon only, so taking that one Pokémon is enough). Step trigger x 1297–1302, z 160 (var 0x409e = 0); side lanes x≈1291–1293 and x≈1306–1308; house door 1297,171.

## Underground Path: the underground battle (story, Cascade Badge)

**Where:** the Underground Path, entered from the Route 5 gatehouse. You need the **Cascade Badge** to take part (the people at the entrance talk about needing "two Badges").

**How it works:** after Blue's win streak you fight a string of opponents: Blue, then four challengers, then Green (Red for female players). Losing to Blue or any of the four challengers whites you out. You **don't have to win the last battle**. If you lose to Green (or Red), she takes the "prize money", and the police raid ends the event either way.

**Reward:** none at the time. Later, the MC is hiding on **Route 6**, at the north end, about 5 steps north and 2 steps east of the Underground Path entrance building's door. Talk to him and he hands over your "prize money" as **1 Fast Ball and 1 Love Ball** (one time only).

*Source:* files 181 (script 3 and the battle script check `CheckBadge 1` = Cascade Badge; script 8, L2293/L2389; earlier losses go to L2379 WhiteOut), 184 (script 1, L629; one-time flag 1866). MC ≈1311,260; Underground Path gatehouse door 1309,265.

## Route 24 / Cerulean City: the burglary case → TM Case → TM28 Dig

**Where:** Cerulean City. The police officer stands 3 steps west of the door of the robbed house, the house north-east of the Pokémon Center. The robbed man is inside that house (it's the **east room of the Cerulean north house**).

**How it works:**
1. Talk to the two suspects: the **bald jogger up north** (at the north edge of town, straight north of the police officer) and the **fat man in stuffed clothes down south** (a few steps south-west of the Poké Mart door). The witness about 13 steps east of the Gym door and Green give hints.
2. Talk to the police officer and name a culprit: "The bald man up north" or "The shady fat man down south". Neither is right. Go to the one you named. The officer follows and clears him. The fat man turns out to be a witness who saw "someone dressed in black".
3. Once you've cleared either suspect, talk to the **dizzy man** in the northeast corner of town, about 20 steps east of the jogger. He remembers a man in black running north, and a Team Rocket grunt appears on **Route 24**, about 8 steps east of the middle Trainer on Nugget Bridge.
4. Beat the grunt. He hands back the loot: the **TM Case**.
5. Back in Cerulean: the officer gives you **2 Cheri Berries**. The robbed man in the north house (east room) takes the TM Case and gives you **5 × TM28 Dig**.

**Deadline:** finish the case before Sabrina laughs in Pokémon Tower ([Vermilion City to Celadon City](03-vermilion-to-celadon.md#pokémon-tower-make-sabrina-laugh-the-marsh-badge-trial)). That scene closes the case: if you haven't beaten the Route 24 grunt by then, the TM Case and TM28 are lost for good. If you beat him but didn't go back to the officer, only the 2 Cheri Berries are lost (the robbed man still takes the TM Case).

**Notes:** you must talk to **both** suspects before the officer lets you name a culprit. Losing to the grunt whites you out; talk to him again to retry. The TM Case is taken from you in step 5.

*Source:* file 756 (scripts 5, 16–19, 23; L3038, L3779, L3807, L2103, L1575), file 215 (script 7, L492), file 761 (script 8, L909). Grunt is trainer 961 (Shieldon 18, Elekid 17, Beedrill 18); loss = WhiteOut (215 L470). Officer L1606 needs both suspect flags 1028 (jogger) and 1029 (fat man). Deadline: the Sabrina scene (file 17 @12111/@12115) sets "case closed" 1024 and hides the grunt (1033); the dizzy man (script 16) checks 1039/1024 first, so his `ClearFlag 1033` (756 @2131) is never reached again. Positions: officer ≈1316,123 (house door 1319,123, the north house's east room); jogger ≈1316,106; fat man ≈1304,149; witness ≈1331,135; dizzy man ≈1337,110; Route 24 grunt ≈1336,79.

*Source:* file 756 (script 3, L1503, L3003). Position ≈1297,127.

## Cerulean City: lend the kid your Bicycle (→ Amulet Coin)

**Where:** a boy in south Cerulean, just south-west of the Bike Maniac's house door (west of the Poké Mart). He's there from the start, but he's away during two stretches of the story: from your talk with Blue at the Viridian Gym (after he becomes its Gym Leader) until Misty wins her Cerulean Gym Leader assessment, and again during the League HQ investigation, from one of your League HQ reports until Misty leaves for the Seafoam Islands ([League HQ: the Trainer Affairs Department's investigations](07-league-to-cherrygrove.md#league-hq-the-trainer-affairs-departments-investigations-where-to-go-next)).

**How it works:** talk to him while you have the **Bicycle**. "Can I borrow your bike for a couple of days?" Answer **Yes** and he takes it. No just ends the talk; you can ask again.

**Reward:** once you have the **Rainbow Badge**, talk to him again. He gives the Bicycle back plus an **Amulet Coin**.

**Timing:** you get the Bicycle from the Cerulean Bike Shop in exchange for the Bike Voucher from the Vermilion Pokémon Fan Club ([Vermilion City to Celadon City](03-vermilion-to-celadon.md#vermilion-city-the-pokémon-fan-club-three-members--bike-voucher)), so you can lend it before the Rainbow Badge; the reward waits until you have that badge. ## Route 25: Nugget Bridge's "tasty treat" (say No)

**Where:** a man on the path north of Nugget Bridge, about 21 steps north of the bridge's last Trainer, who congratulates you on beating the bridge Trainers.

**Where:** Route 25 / Cerulean Cape, during the festival (story).
