## Saffron Gym: Sabrina's willpower test and the pocket dimension (before the takeover)

**Where:** Sabrina at the back of the Saffron Gym. She's only there **after President Goodshow's meeting at Silph Co.** From the Mt. Moon Square scene until that meeting she's away, and the test can't start.

**Who gets it / when:** any time after that meeting and before the Saffron takeover. You can't battle her for the Marsh Badge until Saffron has been freed (below).

**How it works:**
1. Talk to Sabrina. Without the Volcano Badge she calls your will weak. With it she notes your six Badges and asks whether you can resist her powers. Either way, answer **Yes** ("No" just ends the talk).
2. She teleports you into a **pocket dimension**: a copy of Saffron City with invisible walls. You land on the north side, near where the Route 5 gate would be. The Gym door there is locked.
3. Walk to the house in the south-east of the copy (where Mr. Psychic's house stands in the real Saffron). Inside, "Sabrina, and yet not Sabrina", the dimension's caretaker, stands at the back on the right. Ask **"How do I get out of here?"** → **Yes**.
4. You're sent back to the Saffron Gym. Sabrina says she's leaving to train at the **Pokémon Tower** in Lavender Town, and from then on she's gone from the Gym. This is what starts [Pokémon Tower: make Sabrina laugh](03-vermilion-to-celadon.md#pokémon-tower-make-sabrina-laugh-the-marsh-badge-trial). With the Volcano Badge the caretaker comments on your strong will; without it Sabrina adds that Ghost types are her weakness. The result is the same: she still needs the Volcano Badge in the Tower.
**Reward:** a **Mind Plate** item ball in the pocket dimension, a few steps south of the copy of the Silph Co. building (about 34 steps south and 10 east of where you land). The dimension can be entered only through this scene and the takeover teleport, so pick it up now.

**Notes:** during the takeover, Sabrina's teleport from Route 8 drops you in the same dimension ([Pokémon Tower: make Sabrina laugh](03-vermilion-to-celadon.md#pokémon-tower-make-sabrina-laugh-the-marsh-badge-trial), [the Saffron takeover](04-celadon-fuchsia-saffron.md#saffron-city-the-team-rocket-takeover-what-it-closes)). The caretaker then gives you the **Sub. Doll** key item and sends you into the occupied Gym. The Sub. Doll saves you in the Silph Co. finale and is used up there ([the takeover finale](04-celadon-fuchsia-saffron.md#silph-co-presidents-office-the-goodshow-meeting-and-the-saffron-takeover-finale)).

*Source:* script files 826 (script 1 L752, L1257, L1294, L1420; teleport `Warp 405` to 52,40 at L1315/L1485), 830 (script 2, L410, L745, L939; flags 1097/1100, 1588), 838 (script 11, flag 2329). Sabrina's hide flag 1097 is set in the Mt. Moon Square scene (file 9 @5554) and cleared at the Goodshow meeting (file 795 @992); she leaves again at file 830 @923/@1250. Positions: Sabrina ≈15,13 in the Gym; pocket dimension (zone 405) landing 52,40, house door ≈75,82, Mind Plate ≈62,74, Gym door 80,44; caretaker ≈40,5 in the Mr. Psychic house map.

## Saffron City: the Copycat's transformation show

**Where:** the pink house (the Copycat's house) in the middle of Saffron, on the same row as the Poké Mart, about 20 steps west of it. Her two friends wait just outside it. A magician stands about a dozen steps east of the Poké Mart door.

**Who gets it / when:** any time Saffron's townsfolk are out (before or after the Team Rocket takeover, not during it).

**How it works:**
1. **Talk to one of the two friends outside** first. They ask you to hurry her up. Until you do, the girl only says "please don't bother us".
2. Go upstairs in the house and talk to the girl with her Ditto. She wants a performing teacher.
3. Talk to the **magician**. He'll teach her only for a **Twisted Spoon**. There's one in an item ball in the **Magnet Train station**, a few steps right of the entrance; another comes from Route 13. Talk to him again with it and hand it over. He leaves for the house.
4. Talk to the girl again → **Yes** to watch her show. She transforms into you and battles: **Copycat** (Ditto 34, Pikachu 34, Venusaur 35, Charizard 36, Blastoise 36). **Win or lose, the quest goes on.** Losing doesn't white you out.
5. She gives you an **Expert Belt** and goes outside to perform. Talk to her outside to watch her turn into Sabrina (flavour).
6. Talk to the **magician** (now upstairs in the house, next to where she stood). He returns your **Twisted Spoon** and adds a **Silk Scarf**. After that he's the **Extrasensory** tutor (free).
7. Talk to her **father** (downstairs, at the back on the left). He gives you a **Heart Scale** (once).
**Reward:** Expert Belt, Twisted Spoon back, Silk Scarf, Heart Scale, and access to the free Extrasensory tutor.

**Notes:** nothing is missable. If the takeover starts mid-quest, the steps wait until Saffron is freed: the magician is back outside after the liberation (tested in an emulator).

## Saffron City: the Hunyuan Dojo (beat the disciples → Hitmonlee or Hitmonchan)

**Where:** the Hunyuan Dojo, the building just west of the Gym on Saffron's north side. Ma Baoguo stands at the top of the hall, between two Poké Balls.

**How it works:**
1. If you first walk in before the takeover, a scene plays: Master Ma "wins" a sparring match against a young man's Hitmonlee and Hitmonchan. If your first visit comes after the takeover, the scene is skipped. Nothing depends on it.
2. **Talk to Ma Baoguo first.** He tells you to beat his disciples. Until you've talked to him, the disciples won't battle.
3. Beat the four disciples. **Pokéfan Enzo** (right side, lower) fights only after the other three.

| Disciple | Where | Team |
|---|---|---|
| Battle Girl Tina | left side, upper | Lopunny 33, Meditite 34, Machoke 33 |
| Karate King Lincoln | left side, lower | Blaziken 36, Primeape 33, Poliwrath 33 |
| Ace Trainer Tim | right side, upper | Ambipom 34, Heracross 34, Hariyama 35 |
| Pokéfan Enzo | right side, lower | Smeargle 34, Sableye 34, Ditto 34 |

4. Battle **Ma Baoguo**: one **Machamp, Lv. 69**. A loss against him or a disciple whites you out; try again.
**Reward:** pick **one** of the two Poké Balls by the wall: **Hitmonlee** or **Hitmonchan**, Lv. 35. The other stays sealed. Afterwards talk to Ma again for lessons: "Teach me the Lightning Whip!" teaches **Volt Switch** and "Receive, Transform, Release!" teaches **Counter**, $10,000 each (only Pokémon on his list can learn them). **Watch out:** the Lightning Whip is only Volt Switch for a Pokémon that already knows four moves and forgets one; a Pokémon with a free move slot learns **Charge** instead, for the same $10,000 (a hack bug, tested in an emulator; see [the known issue](known-issues.md#lightning-whip-lesson-teaches-charge)). After Saffron is freed his menu also offers "Teach me a little life experience!": just a speech about Team Rocket, no reward.

*Source:* script file 829 (scripts 1, 5, 11, 12, 14–18; flags 1652, 1664–1667, 1698, 1875/1876; the intro is a step trigger at var 0x40c1 = 0, which the scene, the takeover start (file 17 @12105) and the liberation (file 834 @5988) all set to 1; the disciples only check flag 1666, set by talking to Ma (L1086); lesson menu L1094 → L3025, the extra option at L3248 needs flag 1166 (Saffron freed), Volt Switch L3353 (≤ 3 moves → L6363 `SetMonMove` 268 Charge; 4 moves → @4437 521 Volt Switch, D-1347), Counter L3522, speech L3691). Positions: Dojo door ≈1321,204; Ma ≈7,3; Tina ≈3,5, Lincoln ≈3,8, Tim ≈11,5, Enzo ≈11,8.

## Rotom's Room (Silph Co.): change Rotom's form

**Where:** Rotom's Room, Silph Co.'s server room, reached by the Silph Co. elevator.

**How it works:** with a **Rotom** in your party, examine one of the five appliances (microwave oven, refrigerator, washing machine, electric fan, lawn mower) → Yes. Rotom takes that form and learns its signature move (Overheat, Blizzard, Hydro Pump, Air Slash, Leaf Storm). If it already knows four moves, it replaces one. To change it back, examine the appliance it left behind, or use the menu that appears when your Rotom is already in a form.

**Notes:** repeatable, free. During the takeover the room is guarded by four Grunts who battle you when you talk to them (Lv. 63–65), plus two Trainers who battle you on sight: **Executive Lawrence** (Lv. 73–74), right in front of the elevator door, and a Grunt in the south of the room. Lawrence is a real threat, so come prepared. A glowing floor panel in the south-east corner is your way on to the hostages' floor ([the employee's Lapras](04-celadon-fuchsia-saffron.md#silph-co-saffron-takeover-the-employees-lapras-missable)).

*Source:* script file 835 (scripts 2–8, L617, L1352, L2632). Takeover objects (flag 1208): Lawrence trainer 500 at ≈7,4, Grunt trainer 501 at ≈10,11, talk-to Grunts (trainers 626–629, scripts 13–16 → L906/L953/L1000/L1047) at 3,7, 5,6, 9,6, 11,6; floor panel trigger at 13,11.

## Route 19: the surfing Pikachu (Pidgeot lead → Surf for Pikachu)

**Where:** Route 19, south of the Fuchsia gatehouse. A kid with a Pikachu stands on the beach about 15 steps south of the gatehouse exit. The "little beach at the south end" is far down the route, reached by Surf.

**Who gets it / when:** after the Soul Badge. Between the Rainbow Badge and the Soul Badge Route 19 is closed ("volcanic ash"), and the kid waits in the gatehouse.

**How it works:**
1. Talk to the kid on the north beach. He wants big waves so his Pikachu can learn to surf, and suggests a Pidgeot whipping up gales on the south beach. Talking to him arms the scene.
2. Surf to the little south beach with a **Pidgeot** as your first healthy party Pokémon. Stepping onto the right spot on that beach asks "Have Pidgeot whip up a gale over the sea?" Answer Yes.
3. The kid's Pikachu rides the waves to you. He moves to the south beach for good.
**Reward:** the kid (now standing on the south beach, a couple of steps from the spot) teaches **Surf to Pikachu**, free and as often as you like. Only Pikachu qualifies (not Raichu or Pichu).

**Notes:** the spot does nothing if Pidgeot isn't in the lead, and nothing if you haven't talked to the kid since the Soul Badge. Answer No and you can step on it again. A TM12 Taunt item ball lies on the same south beach, just below where the kid ends up.

*Source:* script file 954 (scripts 4, 8; L354, L502, L1175; flags 1210, 1211; var 0x40ae), 955 (gatehouse); file 853 L3174 closes the route, file 806 L1977–L1993 reopens it. Positions: kid ≈1203,468; south beach ≈1208–1210,505; trigger tile ≈1208,506; kid afterwards ≈1210,505; TM12 ≈1210,507.

## Route 21: break the wild Mr. Mime's barrier (unlocks Iron Head)

**Where:** the northern part of Route 21, the stretch south of Pallet Town, about a third of the way down: a Trainer and his Lairon standing next to a wild Mr. Mime.

## Cinnabar Island: finding the hidden Gym (the riddle restaurant)

**Where:** Cinnabar Island. There's no Gym building: the "restaurant that won't serve out-of-town visitors" is the Gym in disguise. Its door is on the east side of the island, a few steps north-east of the old man with the Fire-type quiz. (The side door on the west, just north of the Pokémon Center, leads into a separate room of the same building, the fossil scientist's, not the restaurant.)

**Who gets it / when:** on your first visit (story; you need it for the Volcano Badge).

**Cinnabar at a glance:** the Pokémon Center is in the south-west corner. The lab is in the north, with the lava edge to its east. The restaurant is on the east side, and the fossil scientist's door is on the west side, north of the Pokémon Center.

**How it works:**
1. In the restaurant, Blue is arguing with the staff. Talk to the waiter. He says the owner went out to play riddles with the little girl from the lab.
2. Go outside to the lab door in the north of the island. A man is telling riddles to a girl, Amber, just in front of it. Talk to him: watch the scene, and he heads back to the restaurant.
3. Back in the restaurant, talk to **Blue**, who is standing in front of the owner at the back of the room. The owner asks three riddles. **Any answer works**: if you guess wrong, Blue or Green/Red supplies the right one (bathtub, a toupee, the Cinnabar Gym).
4. The owner is **Blaine**. His assistant opens the secret door in the back wall to the Cinnabar Gym.
**Reward:** access to the Gym. Blaine gives the **Volcano Badge** and **TM38 Fire Blast**, and registers his number.

**Notes:** a man outside the restaurant, a few steps west of its door, also spots that Blaine owns it. The Gym's sparring partner (Sp. Atk EVs for a Chesto Berry) works like the others ([Gym sparring partners](02-pewter-to-vermilion.md#gym-sparring-partners-ev-training-for-a-berry)).

*Source:* script files 905 (scripts 1, 3; L1264–L3716; flags 1213, 1214, 1216, 1217, 1222), 812 (script 1, flag 1213), 15 (L1303–L1695). File 806 L1999–L2011 sets the starting flags. The riddles start from Blue (object 13, script 1, at ≈38,4); the owner at ≈38,3 has no talk script. Positions: restaurant door ≈1048,499 (leads to the restaurant entrance at 37,10); side door ≈1029,493 (leads to the fossil room entrance at 4,8, warp data of zones 57/412); waiter ≈39,3 or ≈41,5 depending on the scene; lab door ≈1037,484; man outside ≈1043,497; secret door ≈39,3.

## Cinnabar Island: the Detrick lab lockdown (door puzzle) and Mewtwo

**Where:** the lab in the north of the island.

**Who gets it / when:** after the Volcano Badge. Before that the guard at the door turns you away. **This scene is part of the story:** until it has played, Sabrina in the [Pokémon Tower](03-vermilion-to-celadon.md#pokémon-tower-make-sabrina-laugh-the-marsh-badge-trial) only says she's "deep in her training", so come here before you go for the Marsh Badge.

**How it works:**
1. Walk up to the lab door and talk to the guard. A researcher runs out: "the monster in the lab has gone berserk" and Dr. Fuji has sealed the lab with emergency lockdown doors. The guard leaves and the lab is open.
2. Inside, the lockdown doors (red shutters) open from computers. A manual on the wall (upper floor, a few steps south-west of the east shutter) warns that some computers control one door and some control two, and each use **toggles** the doors (opens a shut one, shuts an open one):

| Computer | Doors it toggles |
|---|---|
| 1F, top-right corner | the shutter on the west side of 1F |
| Upper floor, just north of the stairs | the **east shutter** |
| Upper floor, 8 steps east of that one | the **west shutter** |
| East side, past the east shutter | the west shutter **and** the **back-room shutter** (the last one on the west side) |
| Behind the west shutter | the east shutter **and** the **inner west shutter** (between the west shutter and the back-room shutter) |

   **The route that works** (it needs the 1F shutter, the west shutter, the inner west shutter and the back-room shutter open at the same time):
   1. On 1F, use the computer in the top-right corner. Go through the shutter it opens and up the stairs.
   2. Upstairs, use the computer just north of the stairs. It opens the east shutter.
   3. Go through the east shutter to the computer on the east side. It opens the west shutter and the back-room shutter.
   4. Go back north, then through the west shutter to the computer behind it. It opens the inner west shutter (and shuts the east one again, which you no longer need).

## Five Island: the Island Pilgrimage leg (Five Island trials)

**Where:** Five Island (the monks' island). The trials run in a fixed order along the island.

**Who gets it / when:** during the Island Pilgrimage, after the [Seven Island start](06-sevii-islands-indigo.md#seven-island-the-island-pilgrimage-start-and-final-trial).

**How it works:**
1. **Monk at the south entrance:** say you're here for the pilgrimage and beat Sage Huijin (Sunflora Lv. 62 lead).
2. **Monk on the east path** (about 20 steps south of the Elder's hut): he wants proof of your catching skill: *"a round Pokémon that is red and green"*. Catch a **Cherubi** in Five Island's grass and show it to him (party select). Any other Pokémon is refused; retry freely.
3. **Snowy mountain.** The monk at the foot of the mountain tells you it's closed. Choose to push on anyway and beat Sage Huiming. Then the next monk further up stops you; beat Sage Huiwu (Bronzong Lv. 62 lead).
4. **The freezing disciple** at the top, with his Snover: talk to him ("Why are you here?"). He's cold and hungry, and surprised anyone came up, since the Elder forbade it.

**Reward:** beating the Elder passes the Five Island trial. Freeing the disciple is optional for the pilgrimage; it opens the **Mew** hunt ([Mew: the long hunt](#mew-the-long-hunt-five-island-finale)).

**Notes:** a loss in any trial battle whites you out. Just try again.
