## Ilex Forest: Bugsy's bug hunt with Honey (story; the more you catch, the better the reward)

**Where:** Ilex Forest. The scene starts when you walk along the path about a dozen steps north of the south entrance (the one from Azalea Town). Bugsy and his team wait a few steps east of that path.

**Who gets it / when:** everyone, on the Ilex Forest chapter (after Slowpoke Well). Saying you won't help only makes Bugsy ask again, so you have to accept.

**Before you start:** if you want the top reward, **don't take the Rocky Helmet** from the man with the sore head yet (see [Ilex Forest: small extras](#ilex-forest-small-extras)). Finish the hunt first.

**How it works:**
1. Bugsy shows you the trick on a Beautifly and gives you **3 Honey**. More Honey is sold by the Gym Trainer next to him: 1 for $500, 3 for $1,500, 5 for $2,500, 10 for $5,000. The Honey isn't used up when you spread it.
2. Four Bug Pokémon sit near four mossy rocks. Examine a rock with Honey in your Bag → "Spread Honey on it?" → Yes. The bug jumps down and runs straight to the rock. **Where you stand decides the result:** if you're on its path, it runs away (and Bugsy's team rounds it up). If you aren't, it licks the Honey and you can talk to it for a wild battle (Lv. 20).

| Rock | Where it is | Bug | Stand here | Don't stand here (it flees) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Kricketune rock | middle of the forest, ten steps south of the Leaf Storm tutor | Kricketune (east of the rock) | west side or south side | north side or east side |
| Ariados rock | about 14 steps east of the Kricketune rock | Ariados (south-west) | west side or north side (the south side does nothing) | east side |
| Burmy rock | far east side, about 15 steps east of the Ariados rock | Burmy (west) | any side except west | west side |
| Skorupi rock | about ten steps south of the Ariados rock | Skorupi (south-east) | any side except west | west side |

3. **Do the Ariados rock before the Skorupi rock.** If Ariados is still up a tree, spreading Honey on Skorupi's rock draws both bugs, and one of them flees.
4. After your **first** Honey, Bugsy moves next to another rock and deals with that bug himself. Your first rock was Burmy's → he takes Kricketune. Any other first rock → he takes Burmy. So you can handle at most **3** bugs yourself.
5. When all four are dealt with, talk to Bugsy (he's back a few steps south-east of where his team stood).

**Reward** (counts the bugs you dealt with yourself: beaten, caught, or run from in battle):

| Bugs | Reward |
|---|---|
| 0 | nothing |
| 1 | Energy Powder |
| 2 | 3 Energy Powder |
| 3 | 3 Energy Powder, PP Max, Heart Scale |

**Notes:** a bug that runs away from the Honey can't be retried. Losing a bug battle blacks you out. Beating it without catching still counts, and so does running from the battle. The best plan: start with Kricketune or Ariados from a safe side, then Ariados before Skorupi, and skip Burmy (Bugsy takes it). Finishing the hunt sends Bugsy back to the Azalea Gym (see [Azalea Gym: Bugsy's no-type-advantage battle](08-cherrygrove-to-azalea.md#azalea-gym-bugsys-no-type-advantage-battle-the-hive-badge)). **Finish the hunt before you talk to Koume, Sakura or the fire ranger:** their quests share the bugs' progress, and finishing the hunt resets it (see the Houndour entry).

*Source:* script file 92 (coord script 22 on var 0x4099 = 1; scripts 2, 4–10, 17, 18; L4209–L10991; reward L2879–L5514; var 0x409f counts catches; wild battles L2069/L2258/L2447/L2542 count any result except a loss; the hunt's end resets the bugs' state variables and the counter). Positions: scene trigger ≈34,60–62, Bugsy's team ≈38–41,60–62, Bugsy afterwards ≈45,65; rocks ≈64–65,49–50 (Kricketune), ≈78–79,50–51 (Ariados; works from x = 77 or y = 49 only), ≈94–95,48–49 (Burmy), ≈74–75,60–61 (Skorupi). The Rocky Helmet man uses the Ariados flag 2104.

## Ilex Forest: Koume, the Kimono Girl who lost her way (blocks the exit to Route 34)

**Where:** Koume stands in the middle of the forest, north-east of the Leaf Storm tutor. Her sister Sakura blocks the Route 34 door of the **north gatehouse** (the forest's north exit).

**Who gets it / when:** everyone. You can't leave for Route 34 and Goldenrod until you've done this: Sakura stands in the doorway until Koume is safe.

**How it works:**
1. Talk to Koume. Whatever you answer ("Go straight ahead" / "Turn north" / "I don't know the way"), she doesn't find the way. The first two make her wander off to the north-east of the forest, about 15 steps south of the fire ranger and his Squirtle.
2. Go through the north exit and talk to **Sakura** in the gatehouse.
3. Go back to Koume (right next to the Ariados rock if you said "I don't know the way", otherwise in the north-east spot from step 1) and choose **"Your sister sent me for you"**. "Find your own way out" does nothing.
4. She follows you. You lead her to the north exit automatically.

**Reward:** **Sun Stone**.

**Notes:** straight after, Gold (badge-race chat) and Crystal (on her way to her grandparents' Day-Care on Route 34) talk to you. This scene **ends several earlier side quests for good:** the Union Cave Cyndaquil ([Union Cave 1F: Goh and the wild Cyndaquil](08-cherrygrove-to-azalea.md#union-cave-1f-goh-and-the-wild-cyndaquil-before-you-finish-the-ilex-forest-story)) and the Violet City Marill ([Violet City: the runaway Marill](08-cherrygrove-to-azalea.md#violet-city-the-runaway-marill--heart-scale-2-pp-max)). It also sends the forest rangers into Ilex Forest.

*Source:* script file 92 (script 19, L3187–L3452, L5832–L10540; var 0x409a 1→2, 3/4 → 5 → 1; flag 2033), file 869 (Azalea–Ilex gatehouse, Sakura: var 0x409a 1→3, 2→4). Positions: Koume ≈75,32; wander spot ≈93,27; "I don't know the way" spot ≈80,49; Sakura ≈37,3 in the gatehouse.

## Ilex Forest: the fire ranger's stray Houndour (→ 10 Safari Balls)

**Where:** the fire ranger with two Squirtle in the north-east of the forest. The Houndour starts in the north-west, about 20 steps south of the north exit.

**Who gets it / when:** as soon as you reach the ranger. Talk to the ranger first; before that, the Houndour only barks. **Safest order: finish Bugsy's hunt, then start this quest.** If you've already caught the Houndour during the hunt, claim the Safari Balls from the ranger before you report the hunt to Bugsy; otherwise the quest resets and the Balls are lost.

**How it works:**
1. The ranger asks you to catch a stray Houndour.
2. Talk to the Houndour. It runs away four times, each time to a new spot. Follow it and talk to it again each time:
   - first next to Bugsy's team, near the south entrance;
   - then just north-east of the Ariados rock;
   - then in the north, just south-east of the man with the sore head;
   - then on the far east side, just south of the Burmy rock.
3. The fifth time it attacks: a battle against a Lv. 20 Houndour. Win, then "Catch it?" → Yes: **Houndour (Lv. 20)** joins you (party needs a free slot; if it's full, come back and it asks again).
4. Tell the ranger.

**Reward:** Houndour Lv. 20 and **10 Safari Balls**.

**Notes:** losing the battle blacks you out. You can retry. Afterwards the ranger tells you a legend about Shaymin, Leafeon and Celebi (a hint for the Forest of Time entry below).

*Source:* script file 92 (scripts 30, 31; L3866–L4198, L6779; var 0x409b 1→9; trainer 764; flag 303). The hunt's end sets 0x409b = 1 (L5725); the ranger's start sets 2 (L4155) and the report sets 9 (L4188). The ranger starts the quest whenever the Burmy state is at its starting value; the hunt's end resets it. Positions: ranger ≈87,12; Houndour ≈35,30 → ≈41,61 → ≈81,48 → ≈64,15 → ≈95,53.

## Ilex Forest / Forest of Time: Sammy and the injured Celebi (League HQ round 3)

**Where:** the old ranger standing by the path into the Forest of Time, a few steps west of its entrance (just north of where Bugsy's team stood during the bug hunt). The Forest of Time is the separate inner wood through that path.

**Who gets it / when:** during the League HQ investigations ([League HQ: the Trainer Affairs Department's investigations](07-league-to-cherrygrove.md#league-hq-the-trainer-affairs-departments-investigations-where-to-go-next)), round 3: "people in black in Ilex Forest". The scene plays when you talk to the ranger at that stage. Before it she only tells the Celebi legend.

**How it works:**
1. A boy, Sammy, falls out of the past. The ranger recognises him from 40 years ago and takes him into the Forest of Time.
2. Follow them in. The injured Celebi needs **10 Fresh Water, 10 Sitrus Berries and a Pokémon that can make light**. The light Pokémon must be your **lead** (first Pokémon that can battle): Staryu, Starmie, Chinchou, Lanturn, Ampharos, Volbeat or Clamperl.
3. Come back and talk to Celebi or the ranger → "Use the prepared items?" → Yes. Celebi recovers (and your party is healed).
4. Team Rocket's Executive Marauder arrives with two Grunts. You hold off the Grunts in a Double Battle (Lv. 62–65) while the others hide.
5. Go north after them. At the end of the path, Marauder (Tyranitar and five more, Lv. 89–90) attacks. You battle him, then Celebi turns time back and you fight him again in a Double Battle against the same team, sent out two at a time.

**Reward:** **Leaf Stone, Max Elixir, PP Max**. The lead is reported to the League HQ desk.

**Notes:** every loss blacks you out, and you can retry. Only Fresh Water and the lead Pokémon are checked. The Sitrus Berries are never checked, and nothing is taken from your Bag (see [Known issues](known-issues.md#ilex-forest-and-goldenrod-city)).

*Source:* script file 92 (script 16, L2672–L5384; var 0x40a2 = 17), script file 52 (Forest of Time: coord scripts 1 and 3 on var 0x4099 = 3 / 5; scripts 2, 4; L2751–L2883, L2885, L3574, L4359–L6148; L437–L2413; trainers 60+62, 763, 763+763; flag 2110). Ranger at ≈40,54 in Ilex Forest.

## Forest of Time: the shrine, Shaymin and Celebi (Gracidea; time of day)

**Where:** the shrine at the north end of the Forest of Time.

**Who gets it / when:** after the Sammy chapter above. **One Shaymin and one Celebi per game.**

**How it works:**
1. **Shaymin:** have the **Gracidea** in your Bag (an item ball in the Cinnabar Island lab; see [Cinnabar Island: the Detrick lab lockdown](05-saffron-cinnabar.md#cinnabar-island-the-detrick-lab-lockdown-door-puzzle-and-mewtwo)), a **Leafeon** as your lead, and examine the shrine between **8:00 and 19:59**. Shaymin appears → "Let Shaymin join your party?" → Yes: **Shaymin Lv. 90**.
2. **Celebi** (after Shaymin): leave through Ilex Forest and come back first (Shaymin's appearance closes the shrine for the rest of that visit). Then lead with **Shaymin** and examine the shrine between **20:00 and 7:59**. Celebi appears → Yes: **Celebi Lv. 90**.
3. With the wrong lead or at the wrong hour, the shrine only shows its description.

**Reward:** Shaymin and Celebi, both Lv. 90 gifts (no battle).

**Notes:** if you answer No, the Pokémon stays on the shrine: talk to it again to be asked again. Once you leave the Forest of Time, it only comes back after you walk back into Ilex Forest itself and return. Your party needs a free slot. The times go by the hour on your DS clock.

*Source:* script file 52 (script 6, L2920–L3940; scripts 7, 8; flags 2296, 2297, 2298; file 92 L130 clears 2298 on each visit to Ilex Forest proper; L2563 checks 2298 first, then 2296/2297; Shaymin's appearance sets 2298 at L3655, Celebi's at L3815 and again after the gift at L3505; nothing clears 2296/2297; the hour comes from ScrCmd_522, compared with 8–19). Shrine at ≈15,14 in the Forest of Time.

## Forest of Time: Celebi takes you back to the S.S. Anne (post-game; only if the ship sank)

**Continues [S.S. Anne: defuse the bomb or the ship sinks](03-vermilion-to-celadon.md#ss-anne-defuse-the-bomb-or-the-ship-sinks-one-chance).**

**Where:** the Forest of Time shrine.

**Who gets it / when:** after your final Hall of Fame entry, after you have both Shaymin and Celebi, and **only if you failed to defuse the S.S. Anne bomb** (the ship sank).

**How it works:**
1. If you've just received Celebi, leave through Ilex Forest and come back first: the shrine stays closed for the rest of that visit. Then lead with **Celebi** and examine the shrine. "Is there anything that happened that you want to change?" → **"I can't watch innocents be killed"**. ("Better not to change history" does nothing, and you can ask again.)
2. You wake up on the S.S. Anne, in the past. Archie is having the bomb fitted to the engine. Battle **Archie** (Walrein, Whiscash, Kingdra, Lv. 43–45).
3. Win and he retreats. Celebi brings you back to Ilex Forest.

**Reward:** the sinking is undone. People in Violet, Olivine and Ecruteak who mourned the passengers now speak as if they were saved, and the S.S. Anne passenger and his granddaughter appear on Celadon Department Store 3F with their Max Revive ([Celadon Department Store 3F: the S.S. Anne passenger's thanks](03-vermilion-to-celadon.md#celadon-department-store-3f-the-ss-anne-passengers-thanks)).

**Notes:** losing blacks you out, and you can try again.

*Source:* script file 52 (script 6 L3336–L3940, L4006–L4959; trainer 177; clears flag 1093); final Hall of Fame = flag 2261; the S.S. Anne branch (L2596) sits behind the shared 2298 gate, which the Celebi gift sets at L3505. Shrine at ≈15,14.

## Ilex Forest: small extras

- **The man with the sore head** (in the north of the forest, about halfway between the north exit and the fire ranger) gives you a **Rocky Helmet**, once. **Take it only after the bug hunt:** he uses the same record as the hunt's Ariados. Taking it earlier makes the Ariados rock do nothing: the hunt still counts Ariados as dealt with and can still be finished, but not as one of yours, so you can deal with at most two bugs yourself and miss the top reward (PP Max + Heart Scale). If you've already done the Ariados rock, he won't give the Helmet until the hunt ends. A Helmet taken mid-hunt means you can get a second one after the hunt.
- **Leaf Storm tutor** (in the middle of the forest, ten steps north of the Kricketune rock): teaches Leaf Storm for a **Leaf Stone**.
- **Forest rangers** (appear after Koume's scene): Ace Trainer Heath, in the south-east about a dozen steps south of the Burmy rock, offers Singles or Doubles as often as you like. Ace Trainer Flo stands just north of the Skorupi rock. **Fisherman Noah**, a few steps south-east of the north exit, is always there and isn't a ranger. Flo and Noah share one "battle done" record, which is set only when you **win**: you get to battle whichever of the two you **beat** first, and the other one is closed after that.
- **Bug Catcher Andy** (north-east of the forest, a few steps north of where Koume first stands): once he has caught the Spinarak that ran away from Azalea Town, he asks you to be their first opponent. Optional battle, no item (see [Azalea Town: small extras](08-cherrygrove-to-azalea.md#azalea-town-small-extras)).

*Source:* script file 92 (scripts 13, 20, 23, 24, 26, 27; Andy ≈78,25, trainer 121, flags 427/1481). ## Route 34: Totodile, the dance master (one chance)

**Where:** a dancer with a twisted ankle on the path between the Day-Care and the Ilex Forest gatehouse, about 25 steps north of the gatehouse. The dance-off grove is at the very south end of Route 34, below the Ilex Forest gatehouse, reached by following the water south.

**Who gets it / when:** after the Ilex Forest chapter (the dancer isn't there before).

**How it works:**
1. Have a **Potion** and talk to the dancer → "Give him your Potion?" → Yes. He tells you about the dance master in the hidden grove. The Potion isn't taken.
2. Walk into the grove. Two dancers try their moves on Totodile and fail. "Go have a dance-off with Totodile?" (No: step back onto the tile to be asked again.)
3. "What kind of dance will you perform?" Only **"Strike a battle-command pose"** works. "Copy Totodile's dance", "A daring moonwalk" and "Give up" **end the quest for good**.
4. Totodile likes you → "Take Totodile as your partner?" → Yes. The two dancers step in: beat them in a **1-vs-2 Double Battle** (Super Nerd Null and Aroma Lady Lydia, Lv. 62–65).
5. Talk to Totodile again → Yes.

**Reward:** **Totodile Lv. 5**.

**Notes:** losing the Double Battle blacks you out. You can retry. You need a free party slot in steps 4 and 5. If you say No in step 4 or 5, just talk to Totodile again later.

*Source:* script file 237 (scripts 9, 14, 15; L3258, L5047, L3288–L5922, L6830–L7071; var 0x408b 11 → 12 → 13; coord script 14 on 0x408b = 11; trainers 766+767; flag 1839). Positions: dancer ≈371,425; grove ≈370,472.

## Route 34: advice for Whitney's victim (→ Razor Claw)

**Where:** a Youngster with a Sunkern in the north-west of Route 34, north-west of the police officer and his Arcanine.

**Who gets it / when:** after the Ilex Forest chapter. The reward needs the **Plain Badge**, and you must claim it **before you earn the Rising Badge**. After that he only boasts about finally beating Whitney.

**How it works:** he's still smarting from Whitney's Miltank. Choose **"Here's how to beat Miltank"** or **"Keep trying, you'll win"** ("Not my problem" does nothing). Without the Plain Badge he waves the advice away, and you can come back. With it, he's impressed.

**Reward:** **Razor Claw** (once).

**Notes:** on the normal path (bug hunt finished and reported to Bugsy) this always works. It can only be lost in an edge case: if you left Ilex Forest in the middle of the bug hunt and earned the Rising Badge before ever reporting to Bugsy.

*Source:* script file 237 (script 16; L3696–L7248; flag 2102; CheckBadge 10 and 15). Flag 2102 is also set during the bug hunt and cleared when it ends. Youngster ≈356,397.

## Goldenrod City: Goh and the Lopunny owner (win with Normal types only)

**Where:** the west plaza at the far west edge of town (the old Wi-Fi center site). A man stands there with his Lopunny; Goh is a few steps south-east of him.

**Who gets it / when:** after the Ilex Forest chapter. The scene is closed by the Route 37 scene with Silver.

**How it works:**
1. Talk to the Lopunny owner. Goh mocks Normal types, and his Blaziken knocks out the man's Lopunny with Close Combat. "Go teach Goh a lesson?"
2. **No:** Goh mocks you as a coward and leaves. The event is over, with no reward.
3. **Yes:** you confront him and he dares you: "beat me with Normal-type Pokémon!" The game checks your party against a long list of non-Normal species. ## Romance route: how it's unlocked, how your partner is chosen, and what locks a partner out (central entry)

Origin HeartGold has a hidden romance route. You pick **one** partner and confess in the final chapter. The scripts then offer post-game dates for some partners only, and moving in appears unreachable for everyone (table in step 4). Four steps decide it. **Steps 1 and 2 are missable and happen mid-game.**

### 1. The love reading (Goldenrod fortune-teller; before the MooMoo Farm investigation)

After the ideal-Pokémon reading (entry above), talk to the fortune-teller again and choose **"My love fortune"** ($10,000; she refuses if you have less). She asks up to five questions. Only one set of answers works:

| Question | Answer that works |
|---|---|
| "Would you be happy to have a companion who secretly adores you?" | **I'd be glad to be liked** ("Just friends is better" ends the reading here) |
| "Could you accept a romance with more than two people?" | **No, I'm loyal to one** |
| "What kind of person are you hoping fate brings you together with?" | **The opposite sex** |
| "At what age do you think it's OK to start dating?" | **After 10** |
| "What do you think matters most in a relationship?" | **Shared memories** or **Mutual attraction** |

- **All correct:** "Your fated love is surely coming in the **near future**." The romance is fully open.
- **Any other answer:** "…may lie somewhere in the **distant future**", or with "Just friends is better", "friendship will be the one and only theme of your adventures." You still pay. **There is no retry**, and in practice every partner ends up locked out at step 2 (see Notes).

**Partner check (optional, repeatable, $10,000 each):** after a "near future" result, "My love fortune" lets you name a friend. Male players: Green, Misty, Yellow, Cynthia, Crystal. Female players: Blue, Red, Steven, Gold, Silver. "The two of you share a special bond" means that partner is still open. "The bond between you two has run its course" means they're locked out. Use it to check your earlier choices (table below).

The fortune-teller stops offering "My love fortune" once you've fought Will and Karen in the Dream World (step 2). **Do the reading before the MooMoo Farm investigation.**

### 2. The Dream World: choose your partner (MooMoo Farm, Route 39; from your first visit)

The MooMoo Farm investigation (sick Miltank, Route 39; [MooMoo Farm: the sick Miltank investigation](10-ecruteak-olivine.md#moomoo-farm-the-sick-miltank-investigation-from-your-first-visit-leads-to-the-dream-world)) is offered from your **first visit to the farm**. The League HQ's round-3 order points you there too ([League HQ](07-league-to-cherrygrove.md#league-hq-the-trainer-affairs-departments-investigations-where-to-go-next)), but the farmer doesn't wait for it. It ends with Will and Karen opening a rift in the stable, and you're pulled into the **Dream World**.

1. You arrive at the south end. **Walk north and talk to the old man first.** He stands on the path north, next to a signpost. He's only there if your love reading said "near future".
2. "Do you like dreaming, young one?" → **Yes**. ("No" ends the talk and he never asks again. He only ever asks once.)
3. "So, who do you usually see in your dreams?" Pick your partner. Male players: **Green, Misty, Yellow, Cynthia, Crystal**. Female players: **Blue, Red, Steven, Gold, Silver**. "No one" picks nobody.
4. Everyone you didn't pick is locked out for good.

Then walk west or east to the story scene: Will catches Cresselia (west) and Karen catches Darkrai (east). They team up against you in a **Double Battle** (Rocket Elites Will and Karen, six Pokémon each, Lv. 90). Losing whites you out. They leave you stranded, and **the old man disappears after this battle**. To get out, examine the broken statue at the north end of the Dream World. It gives you a **Lunar Wing** if you don't have one. Examine it again with the Lunar Wing to return to the MooMoo Farm stable.

**When you leave through the statue without having named a partner** (no love reading, a "distant future" result, answering No, choosing "No one", or doing the battle before you talk to him), **every partner is locked out**.

## Goldenrod City: small extras

- **Gold's slot winnings:** before you record the Radio Tower show, Gold and his newly hatched Togepi are at the Game Corner slots, in the middle of the slot rows. If you have a **Coin Case** he gives you **1,000 coins** (once). Without one he just talks, and you can come back later while he's still there.
- **Bike Shop:** the owner gives you a **Pass** (the Magnet Train Pass to Saffron) as thanks for "beating the bikers outside". You need it at the station gate.
- **Revive from a fan:** a kid standing west of the Pokémon Center, a few steps north of the south Tunnel entrance, gives you a **Revive** because he saw you on TV.
- **Pokémon Center Budew trade:** the girl who wants a Budew is the in-game trade listed under [Goldenrod City's gifts and trades](/locations/goldenrod-city/#special).
- **Flower Shop** (north-east of town, next to the Gym): Saki greets you. If you're **carrying a Gracidea**, she tells you to take it to the shrine deep in Ilex Forest (Forest of Time) to meet Shaymin. With **Shaymin** as your lead she hints that it may lead you to Celebi at the same shrine. With **Celebi** as your lead she praises it. The shop sells herbs, Rose Incense ($9,000), Mulch, and Berry Pots ($5,000) if you don't have them.
- **Daily Drawing (Dept. Store 6F):** $300 a draw. Prizes change by weekday (1st/2nd prize): Sun Heart Scale/Fast Ball, Mon Sacred Ash/Level Ball, Tue Lucky Egg/Lure Ball, Wed Rare Candy/Heavy Ball, Thu PP Max/Love Ball, Fri Old Gateau (announced as a Cherish Ball)/Friend Ball, Sat Max Elixir/Moon Ball. 3rd prize is a Berry. Each draw has a **4%** chance of 1st prize, **36%** of 2nd and **60%** of 3rd. The 3rd-prize Berry is one of Figy, Wiki, Mago, Aguav, Iapapa, Leppa or Oran, with equal chances. There's no daily limit: you can draw as often as you can pay. If your Bag is full you get your $300 back.
- **Pokémon Lottery (Radio Tower 1F):** Felicity, second from the left at the reception counter, draws a lucky number once a day and checks it against the ID No. of every Pokémon in your party and your PC boxes. Prizes by matching digits: 1 digit **Ultra Ball**, 2 **PP Up**, 3 **Protector**, 4 **Max Revive**, all 5 **Heart Scale**. Digits are counted from the end of the number, as in the original games (not confirmed in game). If nothing matches you can ask her to check again the same day, for example after catching or trading for more Pokémon. Once you've won, the next drawing is the next day. If your Bag is full she keeps your prize until you come back.
- **Whitney's photo (post-game):** after the Hall of Fame, between 6 PM and 7:59 PM, Whitney leaves the Gym and shops on Dept. Store 6F. She offers a photo with you.
- **Norman's parenting question** at the station (see the Gym entry) only changes Ruby's lines.
- **Move tutors:** a jogger in the west plaza, south of the Lopunny owner, teaches **Extreme Speed** for one **Lemonade** (from the Department Store). A man a few steps east of the Game Corner door teaches **Belly Drum** for free. (The Hyper Voice tutor by the Gym is in the Gym entry.)

## Dream World: Cresselia and Darkrai (post-game)

**Where:** the Dream World, reached from the MooMoo Farm stable (Route 39; [MooMoo Farm](10-ecruteak-olivine.md#moomoo-farm-the-sick-miltank-investigation-from-your-first-visit-leads-to-the-dream-world)).

**Who gets it / when:** after your final Hall of Fame entry, once the post-game scene at home has sent you off to hunt legendary Pokémon. That scene brings Cresselia and Darkrai back.

**How it works:**
1. Enter the stable by its west door and walk straight ahead to the back wall. Examine the spot there: "The energy reacted to the Lunar Wing you're carrying!" warps you to the Dream World. The game doesn't actually check for the Lunar Wing.
2. **Cresselia** waits on the west side and **Darkrai** on the east side. Talk to one → "Will you catch …?" → Yes → wild battle, **Lv. 90**.
3. Leave through the broken statue at the north end with the Lunar Wing. The statue gives you one if you don't have it.

**Reward:** Cresselia and Darkrai, one chance each.

**Notes:** a win or a catch removes it for good. If you lose, you white out and it stays. Answering No leaves it there.
