## Cherrygrove City: Crystal, the orphanage and Rhett's Slugma (story chain; where to go next)

**Where:** the orphanage, the middle house of Cherrygrove's south-west row (Crystal stands near the top of the room); then the north edge of town, a few steps north-west of the Poké Mart.

**Who gets it / when:** after Crystal's city tour ends at the orphanage door (Cherrygrove chapter).

**How it works:**
1. Talk to Crystal in the orphanage: "Would you let them play with your Pokémon for a bit?" → **Yes**. ("No" just ends the talk; you can ask again.)
2. You introduce your lead Pokémon to the kids. Rhett brags that he saw a Slugma north of town and runs off to catch one. The director asks Crystal to go after him.
3. Go to the north edge of Cherrygrove (north-west of the Poké Mart) and walk up to Rhett, who is surrounded by Slugma. Crystal asks "Will you help hold back the Slugma?":
   - **Yes:** you battle the Slugma (Slugma Lv. 28, 25, 25). Win, and Crystal thanks you with **5 Heal Balls**. Losing whites you out.
   - **No:** Crystal handles it alone. No item.

   Either way Crystal catches the Slugma, and you learn she's a natural at catching Pokémon.
4. Later, back in Prof. Elm's lab with Gold (after the Totodile theft and Silver's dropped Trainer Card), you recommend Crystal as a Pokédex holder. Elm goes to the orphanage.
5. Talk to Crystal in the orphanage again: Elm gives her a Pokédex and Chikorita and promises to fund the orphanage.

**Reward:** 5 Heal Balls if you help hold back the Slugma and win. Step 5 also releases the **wild Chikorita** on Route 30 (next entry).

**Notes:** after the final Hall of Fame entry Crystal is back in the orphanage. She offers a repeatable Singles or Doubles battle. If you've confessed (to anyone) and Crystal hasn't been ruled out as your partner, she also offers "Let's go for a walk together", but that option only plays one line and ends: Crystal has no date scenes.

*Source:* script file 851 (scripts 4, 6; L1271–L2745; var 0x408b 7 → 9, flags 1964, 1965, 1967, 1968), file 847 (L7995–L8091 tour, coord 547,385 → L5553–L5763, L7374 sets 0x408b = 8; Slugma question L1945, trainer 745, Heal Balls (item 14) ×5 at L6549–L6561), file 840 (lab script 3, L1436–L1530: hides the orphanage Crystal (flag 1964) and shows the Elm scene (clears 1968)); post-game: L926 checks flags 1645 (confessed) and 2145 (Crystal ruled out) before the walk option; flag 1964 is cleared again at the final Hall of Fame (file 822 L2522). Positions: Crystal at 34,4 in the orphanage; Rhett at ≈547,385, north exit ≈549,386.

## Route 30: the Stantler roadblock (Noctowl's Foresight)

**Where:** the northern part of Route 30, north of the fishing pond and south of Mr. Pokémon's house: a herd of Stantler blocks one of the two paths north. A man standing just south of them, next to a sign, complains about it.

**Who gets it / when:** from the start of the Johto chapter until the Team Rocket dig on Route 46 (later story), which removes the herd for good.

**How it works:**
1. Put a **Noctowl** first in your party (it must be the first Pokémon that isn't fainted; it doesn't need to know Foresight) and talk to any Stantler.
2. "Have it use Foresight on this mysterious force?" → **Yes**. The herd was an illusion and vanishes. One real **Stantler** is left, hiding in the corner.
3. Talk to it: "Catch it?" → **Yes** → wild **Stantler, Lv. 15**.

**Reward:** the path opens, and you can catch the Stantler.

**Notes:** the lone Stantler is gone once you leave the map, or after the Route 46 scene. If you don't have a Noctowl, use the other path; Hoothoot evolves into Noctowl at Lv. 20.

*Source:* script file 227 (scripts 5, 10, 12; L2453–L2567, L3984–L4149; flag 1807), file 259 L3586 (Route 46 sets 1807). Positions: herd ≈548–553,311–313; complaining man ≈552,317.

## Route 30 → Route 31: the wild Chikorita that challenges Trainers (catch it; one chance)

**Where:** Route 30 (a Youngster with a Rattata, a short way south of the Stantler herd) → Route 31 (a girl with an Oddish near the east end of Route 31; then a Bug Catcher with a Yanma in the west part of the route, towards the Violet City gatehouse).

**Who gets it / when:** after Elm gives Crystal her Pokédex in the orphanage (previous entry).

**How it works:**
1. **Route 30:** talk to the Youngster or the Chikorita beside him. The Chikorita beats his Rattata and runs off.
2. **Route 31:** talk to the Oddish girl (or her Oddish). The Chikorita knocks out her Oddish and moves on to the Bug Catcher's spot west along the route.
3. Talk to the **Bug Catcher first**. He says he doesn't want a Grass type: "If you like it, catch it yourself."
4. Then talk to the Chikorita. His Yanma beats it, and you get "Catch Chikorita?" → **Yes** → wild **Chikorita, Lv. 5**.

**Reward:** a Chikorita. Afterwards the Bug Catcher offers a battle whenever you talk to him and gives a **Pecha Berry** after each win (repeatable).

**Notes:**
- If you talk to the Chikorita before the Bug Catcher, it just lies there hurt; talk to him, then to it again. The Chikorita disappears after the battle whether you catch it, knock it out or run. Losing whites you out.
- **Don't talk to Brock in the Pewter Museum** between the orphanage scene and this event. While his siblings' quiz isn't finished, talking to him removes this Chikorita for good (see [Known issues](known-issues.md#cherrygrove-city-to-azalea-town)).

*Source:* script file 227 (scripts 18, 21, L2586–L4206; flag 1323), file 230 (scripts 7, 15, 17, 18, 20; L1123–L2485, L1700–L2544; flags 1238, 1812, 1813; `WildBattle 152 Lv. 5`; trainer 799, item 151), file 851 L488–L492. Flag 1323 is also set by Brock in the Pewter Museum (file 753 script 16) whenever his siblings' quiz isn't finished (hack finding D-1424). Positions: Youngster ≈552,331; Oddish girl ≈562,282; Bug Catcher ≈531,278.

## Route 30: Mr. Pokémon's quiz (→ Exp. Share)

**Where:** Mr. Pokémon's house, at the north end of Route 30.

**Who gets it / when:** after the Dark Cave incident is over (Charon's scene in Dark Cave, or the Route 46 dig). Before that he only worries about the cave. **Get it before you own HM08 Rock Climb:** once you carry HM08 he only gives a Rock Climb tip and never asks the quiz.

**How it works:** "Answer Mr. Pokémon's questions?" → Yes. He asks five questions in a row and tells you at the end whether any was wrong:

| # | Question | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | How many Pokémon are native to Johto? | **100** |
| 2 | In battle, which of these moves goes first? | **Focus Punch** |
| 3 | Which of these Pokémon could gain an edge after being hit by Gastro Acid? | **Slaking** |
| 4 | Which of these statements is true? | **Forretress has only one weakness** |
| 5 | Which of these effects is NOT caused by weather? | **Attack and Speed both rise** |

**Reward:** **Exp. Share** (once).

**Notes:** a wrong answer costs nothing; talk to him again and retry.

*Source:* script file 229 (script 1; L30 `HasItem 427`, L51 flag 1808 (Dark Cave incident over), L140–L631, flag 2335).

## Route 31: give Crystal your Flash TM (the way into Dark Cave)

**Where:** Route 31, right in front of the Dark Cave entrance (Crystal, with Gold beside her).

**Who gets it / when:** when you reach Route 31, Gold wants to rush into Dark Cave and Crystal stops him because neither has Flash.

**How it works:** talk to Crystal while you carry **TM70 Flash**: "Give Crystal the TM for Flash?" → **Yes**. Her Chikorita learns Flash, and she heads into the cave.

## Sprout Tower: the monks' toll (pay or fight)

**Where:** Sprout Tower 1F, the three monks whose Bellsprout block the way up. You can tell them apart by what they say first.

**Who gets it / when:** you must pass them to reach the memorial ceremony on 3F, which opens the Violet Gym.

**How it works:** each monk asks for an offering.

| Monk | "Yes" (pay) | "No" (battle) |
|---|---|---|
| 1st ("Would you care to make an offering, to pray for blessings…"), near a man worried that the pillar is swaying | **$3,000** | Sage Kongzhi, Lv. 62–65 |
| 2nd ("The upkeep of Sprout Tower is no small expense…") | **$5,000** | Sage Kongxing, Lv. 62–65 |
| 3rd, last before 3F ("ninety miles is only half of a hundred-mile journey"), a few steps west of the stairs to 3F | **$10,000** | Sage Kongwen, Lv. 62–65 |

Either answer moves his Bellsprout aside for good. Losing a battle blacks you out; talk again to retry.

**Notes:** the game takes the money without checking that you have it (see [Known issues](known-issues.md#cherrygrove-city-to-azalea-town)).

*Source:* script file 16 (scripts 8, 10, 13; L973, L1053, L1167; flags 2107–2109). Positions in the Sprout Tower map: 1st monk ≈19,45; 2nd ≈6,10; 3rd ≈8,59 (stairs to 3F at 15,59).

## Violet City: the repentant thief (choice, no reward)

**Where:** a man a few steps south-east of the Sprout Tower entrance.

**Who gets it / when:** only before the Sprout Tower ceremony (it removes him).

**How it works:** he has stolen Pokémon all his life and asks what to do: "Turn yourself in", "Repent as a Sprout Tower monk", "Turn over a new leaf" or "Not my problem".
- Any of the first three answers settles it: he says "Thanks for clearing up my doubts" if you talk to him again, and he's **gone the next time the map loads**.

No answer gives a reward.

**Notes:** he turns up again in Union Cave in the Marill quest below.

## Violet City: the runaway Marill (→ Heart Scale, 2 PP Max)

**Where:** Violet City, the Marill's owner on the west side of town, south-west of the Poké Mart; the Marill at the east edge of town, north of the Route 31 gatehouse. Later, Union Cave 1F.

**Who gets it / when:** talk to the owner any time after arriving in Violet. The quest closes when Crystal's story scene in Ilex Forest plays.

**How it works:**
1. Talk to the owner: she scolded Marill and it ran off.
2. Talk to the Marill (east edge, north of the Route 31 gatehouse) → "Tell Marill to hurry home?" → Yes. It is still angry and moves to the north-east corner of town.
3. Talk to it again and choose:

| Choice | What happens | Reward |
|---|---|---|
| **Talk it into going home** | Marill "finds you annoying" and vanishes. The owner gives you a **Heart Scale**, then leaves to find it herself. Continue at step 4. | Heart Scale (+ 2 PP Max) |
| **Take it back by force** | You drag it home; the owner apologises and gives you a **Heart Scale**. But after the Ilex Forest scene the Marill has run away for good. | Heart Scale |
| **Forget it** | The owner sobs; Marill never returns. | none |

4. (Talk route only) In **Union Cave 1F**, walk up to the owner and Marill in the south-east part of the cave, east of the Route 33 exit. Shady Guy Yamamoto, the thief from Violet, knocks Marill out and throws a Poké Ball. "Step in and stop Marill from being caught?" → **Yes**: battle him (Girafarig, Vileplume, Ninjask, Rhyperior, Lv. 62–65). Win → owner and Marill make up and she gives you **2 PP Max**. Losing blacks you out, and the scene replays on your next visit.

**Reward:** Heart Scale; 2 PP Max for the Talk route with the Union Cave rescue. On that route the owner and Marill are back in Violet straight after your Union Cave win.

**Notes:** if you haven't finished by Crystal's Ilex Forest scene, the quest ends there: the owner stays alone, and on the Talk route, if she has already left for Union Cave, she doesn't come back to Violet at all. In Union Cave, answering No lets him take Marill, but the game still gives the happy ending later (see [Known issues](known-issues.md#cherrygrove-city-to-azalea-town)).

*Source:* script file 854 (scripts 5, 27; L1682–L1830, L2117–L2230, L3780–L3837, L3903–L5163; var 0x40AD, flags 1528, 1824–1826, 1896), file 231 (Route 31 gatehouse L179 sets 0x40AD = 1), file 56 (Union Cave script 5, L785–L989 and L2460–L2874; trainer #756; the return to Violet is set at L2848/L2852 right after the win), file 92 (Ilex Forest L10459–L10467 hide the Union Cave scene and the corner Marill; L10515–L10534 set 0x40AD = 8 and hide the Marill beside the owner unless the var is already 8, else L10927 shows the owner and Marill again; the owner's hide flag 1825, set when she leaves at L3815, is cleared only by the Union Cave win and L10927). Positions: owner ≈463,262; Marill ≈505,254 → ≈505,236; Union Cave ≈51,86.

## Violet City: the Pokémon Academy class Q&A (→ TM67 Recycle or 2 Big Pearls)

**Where:** the Pokémon Academy (the east house on the Gym's street, to the right of the Gym), talk to the teacher at the front.

**Who gets it / when:** any time; one time only.

**How it works:** agree to meet the class. Five students each ask you a question, then Youngster Ward battles you (Sentret Lv. 4, Gligar Lv. 5). Your answers decide the teacher's gift:

| Question | Answer that keeps you on the "Big Pearl" track | Any other answer |
|---|---|---|
| Limber's new effect | "Reduces physical damage" | switches to TM67 |
| How to get Sylveon | "Eevee can become Sylveon?!" | switches to TM67 |
| Did you cheat at the Indigo Conference? | anything except "Yep, I cheated all the way" | "Yep…" switches to TM67 |
| Toughest opponent | any (no effect) | — |
| Pokémon for beginners | "Fast, hard-hitting Pokémon" | switches to TM67 |

**Reward:** keep to the left column all the way → **2 Big Pearls**. Any one answer from the right column → **TM67 Recycle**.

**Notes:** the game uses the same marker for "TM67 chosen" and "gift given". If you give a TM67 answer and then lose to Ward, you black out and the teacher never gives you anything (see [Known issues](known-issues.md#cherrygrove-city-to-azalea-town)).

*Source:* script file 82 (scripts 6–12; L946–L1958; flag 1858; trainer #752). Academy door ≈495,257.

## Violet City: Gyro Ball TM for the Forretress kid (unlocks Shell Smash)

1. Examine the spot → "There's a Cyndaquil up there. Lure it down and catch it?" → **Yes**. Goh rushes in and claims he saw it first.
2. Pick an answer:

| Answer | What happens |
|---|---|
| **"I saw it first!"** | Battle Goh (Manectric Lv. 84, Slowbro 85, Dugtrio 84, Scizor 85, Slaking 84, Tangrowth 85). **Win:** Goh leaves. Examine the spot again → Yes → **wild Cyndaquil, Lv. 10**. Whether you catch it or knock it out, it's gone. **Lose:** you don't black out. Goh chases the Cyndaquil, gets himself trapped behind a boulder, and you get the rock choice below. |
| **"You can have it"** | Goh chases the Cyndaquil, it tricks him, and he's trapped behind a boulder. "Help Goh move the rock?" |

3. The rock choice (after "You can have it"):
   - **Yes:** you push the rock, and Goh catches the Cyndaquil. No Cyndaquil for you.
   - **No:** Goh stays trapped. Examine the ledge again → **wild Cyndaquil, Lv. 10** is yours to catch. Later, walking past the boulder (a few steps south-east of the ledge) brings Goh back: if you already took the Cyndaquil he battles you and leaves. If not, you're asked "Let Goh go catch Cyndaquil?": **Yes** means he takes it, **No** means you battle him.

**Reward:** a Lv. 10 Cyndaquil (your only reward; Goh gives nothing).

**Notes:** the best route is "You can have it" → **No** (you don't have to beat a Lv. 84–85 team). If you lose to Goh in the "I saw it first!" branch, the rock prompt can loop back to the start of the scene (see [Known issues](known-issues.md#cherrygrove-city-to-azalea-town)), so save first.

*Source:* script file 56 (Union Cave 1F): scripts 2 and 6, L1587–L3588, L4012; flags 1980/1981, var 0x40a8. File 92 (Ilex Forest) L10451–L10455 closes it (sets 1980, 1981). Positions: Cyndaquil ≈5,58, examine spot ≈6,58; Goh ≈7,48; boulder tile ≈10,62.

## Union Cave: Heatran in the lava pool (Tuesdays, Magma Stone)

**Where:** Union Cave B1F, the lava pool at the far south end (a pedestal in the middle of the pool). Gold hints on Route 33 that the lower level needs **Surf**.

**Who gets it / when:** you need the **Magma Stone** from Diglett's Cave ([Diglett's Cave: the warm stone in the statue](03-vermilion-to-celadon.md#digletts-cave-the-warm-stone-in-the-statue-after-the-silver-conference)), which you can get from the Silver Conference closing ceremony on. ## Azalea Gym: Bugsy's no-type-advantage battle (the Hive Badge)

**Where:** Azalea Gym (the Spinarak-cart puzzle leads to him).

**Who gets it / when:** Bugsy is away in Ilex Forest until you finish the Ilex Forest bug-catching story. Until then the Gym guide says he's out, and Bugsy isn't in the Gym.

**How it works:** Bugsy asks you to fight **without any Pokémon strong against Bug**. He calls it a 4-on-4 match, but the game never actually counts your party, so you can most likely bring six (see [Known issues](known-issues.md#cherrygrove-city-to-azalea-town)). He checks your **party** against a fixed list of 203 species (Eggs are skipped). If any of them is in your party he refuses ("None of your Pokémon can have a type that's strong against Bug").
- The list is essentially every Fire-, Flying- and Rock-type (Charmander line, Pidgey line, Geodude line, Gyarados, Scyther, Pinsir, Skarmory, Volcarona, Talonflame…). It also bans a few you might not expect, because of this hack's type changes and forms: **Conkeldurr, Tangrowth, Grumpig and Cursola** are part Rock here; **Lumineon and Sirfetch'd** are part Flying; **Cherrim** (its Sunshine Form is Grass/Fire), **Rotom** (Heat and Fan forms) and **Shaymin** (Sky Forme, a form you can't get here) are banned as a whole species because one of their forms has a banned type.
- Some Pokémon with those types are *not* on it: **Hippowdon** (Ground/Rock in this hack) and 55 Fire, Flying or Rock species from after Sinnoh. (Altaria and Tropius aren't Flying here: Altaria is Dragon/Fairy and Tropius is Grass/Dragon.) Check your party before you go in.
- His team: Scyther Lv. 80, Escavalier 80, Heracross 80, Volcarona 80.

**Reward:** **Hive Badge**, **TM81 X-Scissor**, and Bugsy's phone number.

**Rematch:** after the badge, talk to him again → Singles or Doubles (Doubles needs two Pokémon). Level 86–87 team, no type restriction. After a rematch win he repeats his "None of your Pokémon can have a type…" line instead of a victory line; it means nothing. After your final Hall of Fame entry he is away on **Mondays, Wednesdays and Fridays**. Before that he's there every day.

**Notes:** the Gym guide by the entrance teaches **Megahorn** once you have the Hive Badge (see the [tutor list](/tutors/)).

*Source:* script file 866 (Azalea Gym), scripts 17 and 2, L367–L4610 (Bugsy appears with flag 2031, set by the Ilex Forest story); file 865 (Gym entrance). Species-list gaps: hack finding D-1426. Party-size check at L367 compares 0x8005 without a GetPartyCount; rematch win lines L4530/L4590 reuse message 22.
